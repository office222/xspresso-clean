Xspresso Board Shell Deployment Checklist

Purpose:
This file captures the final steps for packaging, validating, and deploying the Xspresso board shell component set.

Checklist:
- Confirm the top bar is white.
- Confirm the cup logo stays left of the name/logo text.
- Confirm the logo shrinks cleanly on smaller screens.
- Confirm there is no overlap or stacking at common breakpoints.
- Confirm the business card area remains readable.
- Confirm CTA buttons are visible and clearly ordered.
- Confirm the footer remains simple and uncluttered.
- Confirm the board shell copy matches the Xspresso message.
- Confirm all filenames follow the Xspresso-only naming pattern.
- Confirm no duplicate filenames exist before download.

Recommended release order:
1. Review the header/logo behavior.
2. Review responsive shrinking.
3. Review CTA placement.
4. Review footer/support links.
5. Run one final acceptance pass.
6. Package the approved files for release.

Acceptance rule:
The board shell is ready only when the logo stays inline, the layout remains readable on smaller screens, and the overall presentation feels clean and premium.
