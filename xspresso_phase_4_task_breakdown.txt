Xspresso Phase 4 Task Breakdown

Purpose:
Phase 4 expands Xspresso from a strong solo-user system into a more team-friendly and agency-ready platform.

1) Team and Agency Workflows
- Add team members to campaigns and workspaces.
- Allow permission-based access for editing, approving, viewing, and billing.
- Support shared libraries and shared templates.
- Add role-based actions for owner, admin, editor, and viewer.

2) Integrations
- Plan for integrations with common marketing tools.
- Support import/export to and from other systems.
- Allow webhooks or sync points for campaign, lead, and alert updates.
- Keep integrations optional so the core product stays simple.

3) Advanced Reporting
- Expand reporting beyond basic campaign stats.
- Add performance summaries by campaign, platform, offer, and date range.
- Show usage, monitoring, and credit trends.
- Provide exportable reports for team review.

4) More Automation
- Add deeper automation rules for monitoring and follow-up.
- Trigger actions when ads underperform or lead scores change.
- Support scheduled tasks and saved workflows.
- Allow automation templates that users can reuse.

5) Template Expansion
- Add more template categories for launches, lead magnets, sales letters, VSLs, and platform-specific campaigns.
- Allow teams to save and share internal templates.
- Add template versioning and template notes.

6) Library Improvements
- Improve search, tags, and filters in the library.
- Support reusable blocks, saved ads, saved pages, and saved sequences.
- Add restore, duplicate, archive, and favorite actions.
- Make it easy to start from proven work.

7) Productivity and Scale
- Improve bulk actions for campaigns, ads, and leads.
- Add stronger dashboard filtering.
- Support higher-volume accounts and agencies.
- Keep the workflow fast even as usage grows.

8) Guardrails
- Keep the same original-work rule: users can reuse their own work, not copy others.
- Keep every feature editable and user-controlled.
- Keep credits and usage transparent.
- Keep the product done-for-you, but still in command.

9) Success Criteria
- Teams can collaborate safely.
- Agencies can manage more clients and campaigns.
- Reporting is clearer and more useful.
- Automation reduces manual work without creating confusion.
- The core Xspresso promise still holds: more responses, less software chaos, and users stay in command.
