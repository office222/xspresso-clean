Xspresso Board Shell Master Checklist

Purpose:
This file gathers the final board shell requirements into one reference so the release package stays clean, consistent, and easy to review.

Core rules:
- Keep the cup left of the name logo.
- Use a white top bar background.
- Let the logo shrink cleanly on smaller screens.
- Prevent wrapping or stacking where possible.
- Keep the business card readable and premium.
- Keep CTA buttons clear and action-focused.
- Keep the footer simple and trustworthy.
- Keep copy short, clean, and Xspresso-only.

Required sections:
1. Header / logo
2. Business card component
3. Hero / main promise
4. CTA row
5. Footer / support links
6. Responsive behavior rules
7. Release checklist

Acceptance criteria:
- Logo remains inline at normal widths.
- Logo scales down on smaller screens.
- White header stays clean and uncluttered.
- Buttons are visible and easy to tap.
- Board shell feels premium, simple, and finished.

Next step:
Use this checklist as the final review guide before packaging the board shell for release.
