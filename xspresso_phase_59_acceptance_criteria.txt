Xspresso Phase 59 — Acceptance Criteria

1) Campaign creation and launch
- Users can create a campaign from an offer, URL, or product image.
- Users can review, edit, regenerate, and approve campaign outputs.
- A campaign can be launched only when required fields are present.
- The launch flow clearly shows what is ready and what is missing.

2) Monitoring and alerts
- Active campaigns can be monitored for status, CPC, CTR, and conversions.
- Underperforming ads trigger clear alerts.
- Every alert suggests a next action.
- Users can regenerate underperforming ads before spending more credits.

3) Billing and credits
- Credits are visible in the dashboard.
- Credit usage is logged and explainable.
- Users can see what used credits and why.
- Credits remain the usage currency across plans and a la carte actions.

4) Template, library, and reuse
- Users can save their own campaigns to the library.
- Users can duplicate their own work.
- Users can restore templates and prior versions.
- Reuse should be simple and safe.

5) Dashboard clarity
- The dashboard shows active campaigns, drafts, alerts, credits remaining, and monitored ads.
- Each campaign card has status, performance, and quick actions.
- The dashboard always suggests the next best action.

6) Onboarding and help
- Onboarding guides the user through campaign input, goal selection, and tone.
- Help text and tooltips are concise and useful.
- Empty states and errors point the user to the next step.

7) Platform switching
- Switching platforms shows a clear warning.
- Users can keep ads running, move them to the library, or keep monitoring for a usage cost.
- The platform switch flow requires explicit confirmation.

8) Final handoff readiness
- The system is stable enough for release packaging.
- All core V1 workflows are usable end-to-end.
- Guardrails prevent confusing or unsafe actions.
- The product is ready for final QA and handoff.
