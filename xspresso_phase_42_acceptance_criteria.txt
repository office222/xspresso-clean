Xspresso Phase 42 Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when the following are true:

1. Deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports is functioning.
2. Smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection are active.
3. Recommendation loops are producing useful next actions.
4. Forecasting and performance analysis are available and readable.
5. Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence is working as intended.
6. Shared asset and template marketplace refinement is in place.
7. Workflow coordination is working across major modules.
8. Guardrails and user control remain clear and strong.
9. Credit intelligence and usage guidance are visible and understandable.
10. Dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch-flow polish are present.

Definition of done:
- Users can move through the system without confusion.
- The product stays aligned with the done-for-you, user-in-command philosophy.
- The system provides useful guidance, recommendations, and visibility.
- The phase improves the product without adding unnecessary complexity.
