Xspresso — Board Shell Style and Layout Notes

Purpose:
This file defines the style and layout rules for the Xspresso front board shell and the business card component that appears inside it.

Key goals:
- Keep the header clean and white.
- Keep the cup icon left of the Xspresso name/logo.
- Allow the logo row to shrink gracefully on smaller screens.
- Keep all important content readable and inline.
- Make the component feel premium, simple, and easy to scan.

Header rules:
- Use a white background behind the logo area.
- Do not place the cup image on top of the name.
- Keep the cup and text logo in the same inline row.
- Use responsive image sizing so the header can scale down.
- Avoid dark header bars that make the graphics look pixelated or cluttered.

Business card component rules:
- The business card should act like a compact summary block.
- Include the core promise near the top.
- Include a short benefit line.
- Include demo and pricing buttons.
- Keep spacing generous and clean.
- Avoid too much copy inside the card.

Copy style:
- Done-for-you.
- Fast.
- Simple.
- More responses.
- Less software chaos.
- You stay in command.
- We do the work.

Responsive behavior:
- The header must shrink without breaking line order.
- The cup should remain left of the name logo.
- The business card should stack cleanly on narrow screens.
- Buttons should remain easy to tap.
- Text should remain readable without forcing horizontal scrolling.

Implementation reminders:
- Use inline tables for the header where needed.
- Use max-width and height:auto on images.
- Keep the top bar background white.
- Keep orange as an accent color, not a full-page background.
- Avoid repeated hero copy inside the board shell.

Accepted visual direction:
- White background.
- Charcoal / black text.
- Orange accent for emphasis.
- Clean spacing.
- Minimal clutter.
- Premium, calm, and easy to use.

Notes:
This file is intended to support the front board shell design and keep the logo/header from wrapping awkwardly when the page is resized.
