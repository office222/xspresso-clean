Customer features
Xspresso isn’t about more software. It’s about marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
These are the things you will actually care about.
Feature 01
Landing page creation with shopping cart for each campaign
AI will build a dedicated landing page for every product, offer, or campaign. Each page can include its own cart so customers can buy directly from the campaign instead of getting bounced somewhere else.
Feature 02
Use your own PayPal or Stripe account
Connect your own payment account so money goes directly to you. You stay in control of pricing, payouts, and customer flow without being locked into someone else’s system.
Feature 03
AI-created ads
Generate ad copy quickly without starting from a blank page. Xspresso can help you create different headlines, angles, and calls to action so you have more to test and launch faster. As well as custom generated graphics.
Feature 04
Adapts ads for each destination
Prepares widescreen, vertical, and square media plus titles, captions, descriptions, hashtags, blog posts, Reddit discussion drafts, and other text-based content.
Feature 05
Direct upload to social media
Once your content is ready, send it to social platforms faster instead of copying and pasting everything by hand. This keeps campaigns moving and reduces mistakes when you are in a hurry.
Feature 06
Demo mode with sample data
You will see exactly how Xspresso works before you commit to a full account. A demo with sample data gives you an instant feel for the workflow and makes the product easier to understand.
Feature 07
Split testing for headlines, offers, and pages
Compare different versions of your marketing to see what performs best. Test headlines, layouts, or calls to action so you can stop guessing and start improving on results.
Feature 08
Brand templates and presets
Start from ready-made templates instead of building every campaign from scratch. This saves time, makes Xspresso easier to use, and helps you stay consistent from one launch to the next.
Feature 09
Lead capture and follow-up
Collect leads and move them into a follow-up flow so customers can keep the conversation going. This helps turn traffic into something usable instead of letting it disappear after one click.
Feature 10
Analytics and performance tracking
You will see exactly what is working instead of making a guess. Track which campaigns, pages, or ads get attention so you can focus on results and keep improving over time.
