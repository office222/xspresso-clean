- Keep the layout FrontPage-friendly and simple.
- Prevent wrapping/stacking surprises.

- Orange only as an accent.
- No dark header behind the logo.
- Keep spacing clean and simple.

Acceptance checks:
- Cup stays left of the name.
- Logo stays on one line as long as possible.
