Xspresso Phase 13 — Acceptance Criteria

Purpose:
This phase is complete when the orchestration layer can coordinate the major modules of Xspresso in a reliable, user-friendly way.

Acceptance Criteria:

1. Final orchestration layer works
- The system can coordinate campaign generation, monitoring, alerts, billing, credits, lead flow, and library actions.
- The orchestration layer can pass data between modules without breaking the user flow.

2. Cross-module intelligence works
- Xspresso can use campaign, alert, credit, and lead data together to make better recommendations.
- The system can suggest the next best action based on the current state of the campaign.

3. Smarter recommendations work
- Xspresso can recommend actions such as regenerate, tweak, pause, monitor, or upgrade based on usage and performance.
- Recommendations are clear, simple, and aligned with saving time and credits.

4. Scheduled summaries work
- The system can generate scheduled summaries for campaigns, alerts, usage, and performance.
- Summaries are short, readable, and actionable.

5. Workflow coordination works
- Campaigns, leads, credits, alerts, and billing can be coordinated cleanly.
- Users can move from one action to the next without confusion.

6. Safety guardrails work
- Users remain in control.
- The system does not overwrite important actions without confirmation.
- All major actions have clear warnings and approvals.

7. User experience stays simple
- The system does not become harder to use as intelligence is added.
- The interface remains fast, clear, and easy to follow.

8. Product alignment is maintained
- The phase must continue to support the core Xspresso promise:
  done-for-you marketing, fast, with the user still in command.

Definition of Done:
Phase 13 is complete when Xspresso can coordinate its major systems intelligently, provide useful next-step recommendations, and keep the user experience clear, safe, and simple.
