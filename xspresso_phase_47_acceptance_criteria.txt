Xspresso Phase 47 Acceptance Criteria

1) Final refinement and stabilization
- Campaign creation and launch flows are stable and usable.
- Monitoring, alerts, billing, templates, and dashboard flows do not block normal use.

2) Campaign creation and launch polish
- Users can build a campaign, review it, approve it, and launch it without confusion.
- Missing required fields are clearly flagged before launch.
- Launch flow provides a clear readiness check and next step.

3) Monitoring and alert tuning
- Underperforming ads generate clear warnings.
- Alerts suggest the next best action.
- Users can regenerate, tweak, or pause based on the alert.

4) Cross-campaign learning refinement
- Campaign data, alerts, and performance history are reflected consistently in the system.
- Recommendations improve based on prior campaign activity.

5) Billing and credit clarity
- Credits, usage, and plan limits are visible and understandable.
- Users can see where credits were spent.
- Upgrade suggestions appear only when they can save the user money.

6) Template and library workflow polish
- Users can save, reuse, duplicate, export, and restore their own work.
- Library and template actions behave consistently.

7) Dashboard clarity improvements
- Dashboard shows active campaigns, alerts, credits remaining, and monitored ads clearly.
- Each campaign card provides status and quick actions.

8) Support and help text cleanup
- Help, tooltips, and empty states are clear and friendly.
- Error messages suggest the next action instead of stopping the user.

9) Release readiness and final QA
- The full flow works from onboarding to launch to monitoring.
- Core copy matches the product promise.
- No major blockers remain for the Phase 47 scope.

Definition of done
- Xspresso Phase 47 is complete when the product feels polished, understandable, and stable across campaign creation, launch, monitoring, billing, and reuse.
