Xspresso Phase 53 — Scope

Purpose:
Continue the final stabilization and refinement of Xspresso so the V1 product is ready for release, with all core flows working cleanly and consistently.

Scope:
- Campaign creation and launch flow refinement
- Monitoring and alert tuning
- Billing and credit clarity
- Template, library, and reuse workflow polish
- Dashboard clarity improvements
- Support/help text cleanup
- Final QA and release readiness

Key outcomes:
- Users can create, review, approve, launch, and monitor campaigns without confusion
- Users understand credits, usage, and a la carte behavior clearly
- Platform switch warnings and monitoring rules remain clear and actionable
- Dashboard and alerts present the right next step at a glance
- The product feels stable, cohesive, and launch-ready

Guardrails:
- Keep the experience simple
- Keep the user in command
- Keep all tools included in all plans
- Use credits for fair usage, not feature bait-and-switch
- Recommend better actions when ads underperform

Success criteria:
- Core flows are stable
- Wording is consistent across screens
- Monitoring rules are understandable
- Billing and credits are transparent
- Dashboard guidance is clear
- The release feels polished and ready for users
