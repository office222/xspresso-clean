Xspresso Phase 61 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when the following are true:

1) Orchestration and workflow
- Campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports work together without breaking the user flow.
- Cross-campaign learning and trend detection are active and stable.
- Recommendation loops provide useful next steps, not vague suggestions.

2) Forecasting and intelligence
- Forecasting and performance analysis are available and readable.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence remains consistent across workspaces.
- Shared asset and template marketplace behavior is reliable.

3) Dashboard and usability
- The dashboard is clear and easy to scan.
- Monitoring and alert information is understandable at a glance.
- Users can see what needs attention and what to do next.

4) Credits and usage
- Credit intelligence is visible and understandable.
- Usage guidance is clear enough to prevent confusion.
- The system explains credits before users spend them.

5) Onboarding and help
- Onboarding is simple.
- Help text, tooltips, and support copy are clean and useful.
- New users can understand the flow without outside help.

6) Release readiness
- Core flows are stable.
- Major bugs are resolved.
- The product is ready for handoff, internal use, or the next build stage.

Definition of done:
Xspresso can guide users through campaign creation, monitoring, billing, templates, and reporting with clear feedback, stable behavior, and understandable next actions.
