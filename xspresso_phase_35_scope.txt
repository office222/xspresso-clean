Xspresso Phase 35 Scope

This phase covers:
- deeper orchestration and optimization across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- stronger recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- improved workflow coordination
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- continued refinement of dashboard, monitoring, and alerts

Success criteria:
- Xspresso keeps feeling faster, smarter, and easier to use
- users always have a clear next action
- recommendations remain helpful and not pushy
- credit usage stays understandable and fair
- the system continues to support done-for-you marketing while keeping users in command
