XSPRESSO V1 FEATURES

Core Positioning
- Done-for-you marketing that gets responses.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.

V1 Scope
1. Campaign Creation
- Accept product photo, URL, or offer details.
- Research matching products and competing offers.
- Generate ads, hooks, headlines, campaign angles, landing page copy, cart copy, lead magnet copy, and follow-up emails.

2. Platform Adaptation
- Adapt each ad and asset to the destination platform.
- Support platform-specific sizes, formats, and text variations.

3. Landing Pages / Shopping Cart
- Build landing pages for each campaign.
- Include shopping cart or lead magnet options.
- Keep pages editable before launch.

4. Lead Capture and Follow-Up
- Capture leads from campaigns.
- Send automatic follow-up messages.
- Support campaign-level autoresponder logic.

5. Monitoring
- Watch ads, CPC, and campaign stats.
- Alert the user when something is off.
- Suggest next steps when performance drops.

6. Credits / Usage
- All plans include every tool.
- Credits determine fair usage.
- No feature bait-and-switch.

7. Underperforming Ad Logic
- Warn when an ad is underperforming.
- Recommend a new title, hook, angle, or CTA.
- Allow one-click regeneration.

8. Platform Switching
- Show a clear warning when switching platforms.
- Offer keep-running, move-to-library, or continue-monitoring options.
- Support daily, weekly, or monthly monitoring charges.

9. Campaign Library
- Save, reuse, duplicate, export, and import user-owned campaigns.
- Support version history and restore points.
- Allow templates and presets.

10. Dashboard
- Show active campaigns, drafts, alerts, credits remaining, and monitored ads.
- Provide quick actions for edit, duplicate, pause, regenerate, export, and monitor.

Core Rules
- Keep it fast.
- Keep it simple.
- Keep it customer-focused.
- Keep the user in command.
- Make it easy to approve, tweak, regenerate, and launch.
