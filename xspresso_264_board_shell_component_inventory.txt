Xspresso Board Shell Component Inventory

Purpose:
This file captures the reusable pieces of the board shell so the layout can be rebuilt or adjusted without hunting through multiple notes.

Core Components:
1. White Top Bar
- White background only
- Cup icon stays left of the name/logo text
- No stacking or overlap on shrink
- Responsive inline behavior

2. Business Card Panel
- Main brand card in the board shell
- Short positioning line
- Short support line
- Demo and pricing buttons
- Clean, premium spacing

3. Hero Area
- Done-for-you marketing message
- Wake up your marketing line
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.

4. Primary CTA Row
- Try the Demo
- See Pricing
- Buttons should remain clearly separated
- Buttons should stay readable on smaller screens

5. Footer / Support Strip
- Simple and light
- Support or help-center links
- No clutter
- No competing messages

6. Responsive Rules
- Logo stays inline
- Cup remains left of name
- Text scales down before wrapping
- Avoid overlap at tablet widths
- Maintain white background in header

7. Copy Rules
- Keep language clear and direct
- Avoid too much hype
- Emphasize done-for-you
- Emphasize user control
- Emphasize time savings
- Emphasize less software chaos

8. Acceptance Checklist
- Header is white
- Cup remains left of name
- Logo shrinks without wrapping
- Business card remains readable
- CTA buttons are visible and distinct
- Footer stays simple
- Layout feels premium and clean

Suggested File Use:
Use this file as the quick reference when editing the board shell or rebuilding the top of the page.
