Xspresso — Business Card Component Notes

Purpose:
Create a small, premium, easy-to-scan business card style component for the Quote3 front board shell.

Goals:
- Keep it visually clean and compact.
- Match the Xspresso brand colors and tone.
- Make the message clear at a glance.
- Fit well inside the front board shell without crowding.

Suggested content:
- Brand name: Xspresso
- Tagline: Done-for-you marketing
- Short support line: Wake up your marketing.
- Control line: You stay in command. We do the work.
- Optional note: More responses. Less software chaos.

Design direction:
- Use white or light background.
- Use orange as an accent, not a full background.
- Keep typography bold but simple.
- Maintain good spacing around the logo and text.
- Ensure the component remains readable when the page is resized.

Recommended layout:
- Left: cup/logo graphic
- Right: brand name and short copy
- Optional small footer line below

Mobile behavior:
- Keep elements inline on larger screens.
- Allow safe scaling on smaller screens.
- Avoid overlapping the cup and name.
- Prioritize readability over decorative effects.

Helpful copy options:
- Xspresso — Done-for-you marketing
- Wake up your marketing.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.

Implementation note:
This component should be simple to drop into the board shell and should not introduce layout instability when the window is narrowed.
