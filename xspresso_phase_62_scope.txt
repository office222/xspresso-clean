Xspresso Phase 62 Scope

This phase continues the final refinement and stabilization of Xspresso.

Covers:
- deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- stronger recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- improved workflow coordination
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- continued polish for dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch flow
- release hardening and handoff readiness
- final system consistency checks

Success criteria:
- major flows remain stable
- alerts stay useful and actionable
- credits and billing remain clear
- campaign creation stays fast and simple
- monitoring continues to guide next actions
- templates, library, and reuse stay clean
- dashboard remains easy to understand
- the product feels ready for real users
