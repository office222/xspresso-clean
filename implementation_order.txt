Xspresso Implementation Order

1. Core campaign builder
- Accept product photo, URL, or offer details
- Generate headlines, hooks, ads, landing page copy, cart/lead capture copy, and follow-up

2. Review and approval flow
- Let the user edit, approve, or regenerate each section
- Keep the user in command

3. Launch flow
- Check readiness before launch
- Confirm required assets are present
- Launch the campaign

4. Monitoring engine
- Track CPC, CTR, conversions, ad status, and performance trends
- Show alerts when ads underperform or CPC rises

5. Regeneration logic
- Offer one-click regeneration when an ad underperforms
- Suggest new title, hook, angle, or CTA

6. Lead capture and follow-up
- Capture leads from campaigns
- Score and tag leads
- Send campaign-level follow-up sequences

7. Credits and usage
- All tools available on every plan
- Credits determine fair usage
- Ongoing monitoring and a la carte actions consume credits

8. Platform switching
- Show a strong warning when switching platforms
- Offer keep tracking, move to library, or track myself options
- Support daily, weekly, monthly monitoring charges

9. Library and duplication
- Save campaigns to library
- Duplicate user-owned campaigns only
- Export and import templates

10. Dashboard and alerts
- Show active campaigns, alerts, credits, and recent activity
- Provide clear next actions

11. Billing
- Track plan, renewal, usage, and upgrades
- Suggest upgrade when it may save money

12. V2 items
- Sales letters
- VSL scripts
- Video production
- Long-form funnels
- Advanced campaign cloning of user-owned work
