Xspresso Phase 5 Scope

Phase 5 is about expansion, ecosystem, and scale.

Goals:
- Add collaboration features for teams
- Add permissions and roles for larger organizations
- Add webhooks and integrations for automation tools
- Add reporting exports and scheduled reports
- Add workflow automation between campaigns, alerts, leads, and billing
- Add more advanced template management
- Add marketplace-style sharing for user-created templates and presets
- Add enterprise-level controls for high-volume users

Phase 5 guardrails:
- Keep the core promise simple: done-for-you marketing, fast
- Do not lose the user-in-command model
- Do not lock core tools behind confusing feature walls
- Keep credits, usage, and fair billing visible
- Keep originality rules in place: strategy-inspired, never copy
- Keep monitoring, alerts, and recommendations useful, not noisy

Success criteria:
- Teams can collaborate cleanly
- Users can automate more of their workflow
- Reporting becomes easier to export and share
- The platform scales without losing clarity
- Xspresso still feels fast, simple, and customer-focused
