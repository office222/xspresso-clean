XSPRESSO MASTER INDEX

Purpose:
Xspresso is a done-for-you marketing system that helps users create campaigns faster, build lists, follow up automatically, and stay in command.

Core positioning:
- Done-for-you marketing, fast
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.
- All plans include every tool. Credits simply determine fair usage.

Build principles:
1. Keep it simple.
2. Keep it fast.
3. Keep it customer-focused.
4. Show all tools on all plans.
5. Use credits for fair usage.
6. Warn the user before spending credits on underperforming work.
7. Let users approve, edit, or regenerate without starting over.
8. Keep users in command while Xspresso does the work.

Notes:
- V1 stays focused on campaign creation, monitoring, lead capture, follow-up, and credits.
- V2 can later add sales letters, VSLs, cloning of user-owned work, and video production.
- Platform switching should always warn users clearly and let them choose what happens to ads.
