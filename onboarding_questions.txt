Xspresso V1 — Onboarding Questions

Purpose:
Collect just enough information to build a useful first draft without overwhelming the user.

Core questions:
1. What are you selling?
   - Product name
   - Product image
   - Product URL
   - Short description

2. Who is it for?
   - Audience description
   - Customer pain point
   - Desired outcome

3. What do you want Xspresso to build?
   - Landing page
   - Ad campaign
   - Lead magnet
   - Follow-up sequence
   - Full campaign

4. What is your main goal?
   - Clicks
   - Leads
   - Sales
   - List growth
   - Follow-up automation

5. What tone do you want?
   - Simple
   - Friendly
   - Bold
   - Premium
   - Direct
   - Persuasive

6. Where will this campaign run?
   - Platform name
   - Upload destination
   - Monitoring needed or not

7. Do you want a template?
   - Start fresh
   - Use a preset
   - Use a saved campaign

8. Do you want Xspresso to monitor it after launch?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Yes, with credits

Helpful UX rule:
- If the user skips a question, Xspresso should continue with smart defaults.
- Never block the user unless required fields are missing.

Friendly copy:
- Tell Xspresso as much as you can.
- If you do not know, Xspresso can still build a draft.
- You stay in command.
- Xspresso does the heavy lifting.

Best one-line summary:
Xspresso should ask only the questions needed to create a strong first draft, then fill in the rest with smart defaults.
