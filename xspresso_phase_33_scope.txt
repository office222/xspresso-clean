Xspresso Phase 33 Scope

This phase continues the long-range expansion of Xspresso with a focus on:
- deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- stronger multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- improved shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- tighter workflow coordination
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- continued polish for dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and billing
- stronger guardrails and user control

Success criteria:
- the system continues to feel simple, useful, and done-for-you
- the user stays in command
- the platform keeps reducing wasted time and wasted effort
- monitoring, credits, and recommendations remain transparent and helpful
- the product remains aligned with the core Xspresso promise
