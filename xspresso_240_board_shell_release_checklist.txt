XSPRESSO BOARD SHELL RELEASE CHECKLIST

Purpose:
This file keeps the Quote3 / board shell build clean, consistent, and ready for final integration.

1) Header / Logo Rules
- Keep the cup logo to the left of the Xspresso name.
- Use a white background behind the logo area.
- Prevent the cup from stacking above the name on smaller screens.
- Allow the logo to shrink inline when space is limited.
- Keep the top bar clean and premium.

2) Business Card Rules
- Keep the business card readable and simple.
- Use a clear headline, short support line, and two CTA buttons.
- Keep the copy focused on done-for-you marketing.
- Use short, easy-to-scan text.
- Avoid clutter and repetition.

3) CTA Rules
- Primary CTA should be the main action.
- Secondary CTA should be smaller and less visually dominant.
- Keep CTA language short and direct.
- Use action-oriented language like Try the Demo, See Pricing, Launch, Save Draft, or Export.

4) Responsive Rules
- The header must stay inline as the screen shrinks.
- The cup and name should remain side by side as long as possible.
- The layout should not feel crowded on mobile.
- Use white space and clean spacing.
- Avoid wrap surprises.

5) Footer Rules
- Keep the footer simple.
- Include only necessary support or trust links.
- Do not overcrowd the bottom of the shell.
- Keep the closing line premium and calm.

6) Acceptance Checks
- Logo stays inline.
- White background stays white.
- Card copy stays readable.
- CTAs are visible and balanced.
- Layout feels polished on desktop and smaller screens.
- No unnecessary repetition.

7) Final Build Reminder
The board shell should feel like a premium software interface, not a flyer.
It should be clean, easy to scan, and aligned with the Xspresso promise:

- Done-for-you marketing
- More responses
- Less software chaos
- You stay in command
- We do the work

8) Next Step
When this shell is ready, move the same design rules into the remaining sections of the app so the whole product feels consistent.
