Xspresso Phase 36 Scope

This phase focuses on final refinement and product hardening.

Scope:
- Final orchestration polish across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- Smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- Stronger recommendation loops
- Broader forecasting and performance analysis
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- Shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- Improved workflow coordination
- Tighter guardrails and user control
- Clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- Continued polish for dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch flow

Goal:
Make the platform feel cohesive, reliable, and simple to operate while preserving the done-for-you promise and user control.

Success criteria:
- Users can launch, monitor, improve, and reuse campaigns without confusion
- Alerts and recommendations remain clear and actionable
- Credits, usage, and plan behavior remain transparent
- The app feels fast, clean, and dependable
