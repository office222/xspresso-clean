Xspresso Phase 40 — Acceptance Criteria

This file defines the acceptance criteria for Phase 40 of Xspresso.

The Phase 40 work is complete when:

- Campaigns can be orchestrated across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Cross-campaign learning and trend detection are functioning.
- Recommendation loops are producing useful next-step suggestions.
- Forecasting and performance analysis are available and readable.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence is operating properly.
- Shared asset and template marketplace refinement is in place.
- Workflow coordination is working across the key modules.
- Guardrails and user control are preserved.
- Credit intelligence and usage guidance are clear.
- Dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch-flow polish are complete.

Definition of done:
- The system behaves consistently.
- The user can understand what Xspresso is doing.
- The user can act on recommendations.
- The user remains in command.
- The experience stays aligned with the Xspresso core promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, with less software chaos.
