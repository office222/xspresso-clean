Xspresso FAQ

1. What is Xspresso?
Xspresso is a done-for-you marketing system that helps users create campaigns faster, build lists, follow up automatically, and stay in command.

2. Is Xspresso just another software tool?
No. Xspresso is designed to replace software chaos with one simple workflow for campaign creation, monitoring, lead capture, and follow-up.

3. Do all plans include all tools?
Yes. All plans include every tool. Credits simply determine fair usage.

4. What are credits used for?
Credits are used for actions such as generating campaigns, adding landing pages, creating ad variations, monitoring ads, uploading to platforms, and other usage-based actions.

5. What happens when credits run low?
Xspresso should warn the user before credits run out and suggest an upgrade if it could save money.

6. Can I monitor my ads on a platform I already use?
Yes. If you connect a platform, Xspresso can monitor existing ads on that platform where supported.

7. What happens when I switch platforms?
Xspresso should warn you that monitoring on the old platform will stop unless you choose to keep it active for a usage cost.

8. Can I keep specific ads running?
Yes. Users can choose to keep specific ads running, move them to their library, or keep monitoring them for credits.

9. What does move to library mean?
It means the ad is removed from the platform and saved in your Xspresso library for later review, reuse, or restore.

10. What happens if an ad underperforms?
Xspresso should warn you that the ad is underperforming and recommend improvements such as a new title, hook, angle, or CTA.

11. Can Xspresso regenerate an ad?
Yes. Xspresso can regenerate an ad with a new title, hook, angle, or related improvement before more credits are spent.

12. Does Xspresso build landing pages too?
Yes. Landing pages are part of the core V1 workflow.

13. Does Xspresso build follow-up emails?
Yes. Xspresso can help build lead capture and follow-up flows so traffic can turn into a list and a list can turn into sales.

14. Can I reuse my own campaigns?
Yes. Xspresso lets users save, duplicate, export, import, and reuse their own campaigns and templates.

15. Is there a V2?
Yes. V2 ideas include sales letters, VSLs, video production support, and deeper long-form funnel tools.

16. What is the core promise?
Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.

17. Why was Xspresso built?
Xspresso was built to help people avoid the pain of wasted money, wasted time, and too much software juggling.

18. Can I customize the tone?
Yes. The user should be able to choose tones like simple, friendly, bold, premium, direct, or persuasive.

19. Is Xspresso beginner-friendly?
Yes. The workflow should allow users to start with very little input and still get a usable draft.

20. What if I need help?
Xspresso should include a help center, support topics, and simple in-app guidance so users can keep moving.
