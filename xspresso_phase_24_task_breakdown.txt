Xspresso Phase 24 — Task Breakdown

1) Deeper autonomous campaign orchestration
- Coordinate campaigns across ads, landing pages, follow-up, alerts, billing, and templates.
- Keep the system focused on moving users from idea to launch with less manual work.

2) Smarter cross-campaign learning
- Reuse performance patterns from one campaign to inform the next.
- Identify repeating wins and weak points across multiple campaigns.

3) Stronger recommendation loops
- Suggest the next best action when performance changes.
- Recommend tweaks to title, hook, angle, CTA, or creative when needed.

4) Broader forecasting and trend analysis
- Surface upcoming risks or opportunities based on recent behavior.
- Show simple trend direction for clicks, leads, conversions, CPC, and engagement.

5) Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- Support multiple brands, campaigns, and working groups.
- Allow separate views and defaults for different teams or brands.

6) Improved workflow coordination
- Keep campaign, lead, alert, and billing actions synchronized.
- Ensure one part of the workflow updates the rest when changes happen.

7) Tighter guardrails and user control
- Keep the user in command.
- Require clear confirmation before high-impact actions.
- Preserve the "approve, tweak, or regenerate" workflow.

8) Better credit intelligence and usage guidance
- Show where credits are being used.
- Warn when credits are being spent on low-performing actions.
- Suggest a plan upgrade when it could save money.

9) Success criteria
- The system gives clearer, smarter recommendations.
- Workflow orchestration feels more automatic.
- Users keep control without feeling buried in complexity.
- Credit usage and performance insights are easy to understand.
