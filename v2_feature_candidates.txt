Xspresso V2 Feature Candidates

Goal:
Define the next layer of Xspresso after V1 is stable and shippable.

Core V2 ideas:
- Sales letters
- VSL scripts
- Long-form funnel copy
- Video production support
- Campaign cloning of the user’s own work
- Deeper automation for funnels
- Expanded monitoring and optimization
- More advanced conversion assets

Suggested V2 direction:
1. Copy expansion
   - Sales letter creation
   - VSL creation
   - Long-form page writing
   - Bullet writing at scale

2. Media expansion
   - Script-to-video support
   - Video ad production support
   - Platform-specific video outputs

3. Reuse and scaling
   - Clone your own winning campaigns
   - Duplicate your own pages, ads, and sequences
   - Build variants faster from proven work

4. Optimization expansion
   - Smarter recommendations
   - Better tracking summaries
   - Deeper alert logic
   - More advanced performance guidance

5. Workflow expansion
   - More templates and presets
   - More automation options
   - Larger campaign libraries
   - More advanced export/import flows

Important product rule:
- Keep V2 as an upgrade path, not a distraction from V1.
- V1 must stay focused on fast, done-for-you marketing.
- V2 should deepen selling power and media production.

Suggested V2 positioning:
- Xspresso V1: build and launch faster
- Xspresso V2: sell harder and scale further

Open questions for later:
- Should V2 be sold as a premium tier or separate add-on?
- Should video production be native or integrated?
- Should cloning be limited to user-owned campaigns only?
- Should sales letters and VSLs be bundled together or sold separately?
