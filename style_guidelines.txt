Xspresso Style Guidelines

1) Core Tone
- Clear
- Confident
- Helpful
- Customer-focused
- Done-for-you
- Friendly, not flashy
- Premium, not noisy

2) Key Phrases to Reinforce
- Done-for-you marketing
- You stay in command
- We do the work
- More responses
- Less software chaos
- Wake up your marketing
- More for less
- Time back, not more busywork

3) Copy Rules
- Keep headlines short and clear
- Use bullets where possible
- Avoid long paragraphs at the top of the page
- Use simple words over jargon
- Make benefits obvious fast
- Keep promises believable
- Never imply copying another creator’s work
- Emphasize that users can tweak and approve

4) Visual Style
- White backgrounds where possible
- Burnt orange as an accent, not the entire background
- Dark charcoal for contrast
- Clean spacing between sections
- Avoid clutter and tiny text
- Use strong hierarchy: headline > subheadline > bullets > CTA

5) Button Style
- Short, direct labels
- Try the Demo
- See Pricing
- Approve Changes
- Launch Campaign
- Regenerate This Ad
- Keep Monitoring

6) Error / Alert Tone
- Calm
- Clear
- Actionable
- Helpful
- No blame

Examples:
- This ad is underperforming. Improve it before spending more credits.
- CPC is rising. Try a new headline or angle.
- Switching platforms will stop tracking unless you choose a monitoring option.

7) Product Feel
Xspresso should feel like a smart, practical assistant that helps users move faster without making them feel dumb, trapped, or overloaded.

8) Summary
Keep it clean, fast, helpful, and focused on results.
