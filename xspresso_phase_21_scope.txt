Xspresso Phase 21 Scope

This phase is a continuation of the advanced orchestration layer and focuses on the next level of intelligence, scale, and usability.

Focus areas:
- deeper autonomous orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- broader forecasting and recommendation loops
- stronger multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- more advanced workflow coordination
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage suggestions

Primary outcome:
Xspresso becomes even better at helping users move fast, stay in command, and avoid wasted effort while the system handles more of the repetitive work.

Success criteria:
- orchestration is more proactive and reliable
- recommendations are more useful and timely
- forecasting gives clearer direction
- team and brand controls remain simple
- guardrails continue to protect the user experience
- the system still feels done-for-you, but user-controlled
