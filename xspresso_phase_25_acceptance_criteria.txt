Xspresso Phase 25 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when the system can:

- Coordinate deeper autonomous campaign orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Learn from performance across campaigns and surface smarter recommendations.
- Detect trends and generate clearer forecasting summaries.
- Support stronger multi-brand and multi-team intelligence.
- Improve workflow coordination without breaking the user’s control model.
- Keep guardrails, permissions, and user approval intact.
- Provide better credit intelligence and usage guidance.
- Maintain a simple, understandable user experience even as automation expands.

Definition of done:
- The phase’s orchestration, intelligence, and workflow goals are demonstrably working.
- The user still stays in command.
- Xspresso continues to feel done-for-you, fast, and simple.
