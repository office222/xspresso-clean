Xspresso V2 Features

Purpose:
Xspresso V2 expands the V1 done-for-you marketing system into deeper sales assets, more advanced reuse tools, and richer media creation.

Core V2 Additions:
- Sales letter generation
- VSL script generation
- Long-form funnel copy
- Advanced bullet writing
- Campaign cloning of the user's own work
- Video production support
- Stronger campaign optimization tools
- Expanded automation and workflow controls

Sales Letter Module:
- Generate long-form sales pages
- Create sections for headline, lead, proof, benefits, objections, and CTA
- Support multiple tones and lengths
- Allow section-by-section editing and regeneration

VSL Module:
- Convert an offer into a video script
- Generate opening hooks, story sections, proof, offer presentation, and CTA
- Produce short and long VSL versions
- Support simple scene or slide prompts for video production

Campaign Cloning / Reuse:
- Duplicate the user's own winning campaigns
- Reuse own landing pages, ads, and follow-up sequences
- Preserve the original campaign while creating editable copies
- Clearly prevent copying other people's work

Video Production Support:
- Turn campaign copy into video-ready scripts
- Provide scene-by-scene outlines
- Create platform-specific video versions
- Support ad videos, VSLs, and short promotional clips

Advanced Automation:
- More campaign rules
- More sophisticated follow-up branching
- Better reuse of assets
- Faster duplication of successful marketing systems

V2 Positioning:
Xspresso V1 = done-for-you marketing, fast.
Xspresso V2 = deeper selling assets, video, and advanced reuse.

Guiding Rule:
V2 should still keep the same promise:
- done-for-you
- fast
- simple
- you stay in command
- we do the work

User-Facing Summary:
Xspresso V2 helps users create bigger sales assets, stronger funnels, and video-ready marketing from the campaigns they already trust.
