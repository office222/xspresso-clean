Xspresso Phase 37 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- The app supports deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Cross-campaign learning and trend detection work consistently.
- Recommendation loops provide useful next steps.
- Forecasting and performance analysis are available and readable.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence works as intended.
- Shared asset and template marketplace refinement is supported.
- Workflow coordination is stable.
- Guardrails and user control remain clear.
- Credit intelligence and usage guidance are accurate.
- Dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch flow polish are complete.

Definition of done:
- The phase delivers a stable, usable, and coherent product layer that improves automation, insight, and control without reducing clarity for the user.
