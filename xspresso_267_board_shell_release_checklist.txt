Xspresso Board Shell Release Checklist

Purpose:
A clean, premium, responsive board shell that keeps the cup left of the name/logo, stays white behind the header, and scales without stacking awkwardly.

Checklist:
- White top bar in the header area
- Cup stays left of the name/logo
- Logo shrinks cleanly on smaller screens
- No overlap, stacking, or wrap issues
- Business card remains readable
- CTA buttons remain clear and visible
- Footer stays simple and lightweight
- Layout remains FrontPage-friendly
- Copy stays aligned with the Xspresso promise

Core promise to reflect:
Done-for-you marketing. You stay in command. We do the work.

Acceptance check:
If the board shell looks clean, remains readable on smaller screens, and the brand remains consistent, it is ready for release.
