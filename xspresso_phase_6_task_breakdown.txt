Xspresso Phase 6 — Task Breakdown

1) Advanced automation
- Expand the rules engine for smarter campaign actions.
- Trigger follow-up, alerts, and regeneration automatically when thresholds are met.
- Reduce manual checking by surfacing the next best action.

2) Smarter AI guidance
- Improve recommendation quality for underperforming ads.
- Generate clearer suggestions for titles, hooks, angles, and CTAs.
- Make the assistant more helpful and more specific.

3) Deeper reporting
- Add richer summaries for campaigns, ads, leads, and usage.
- Show trends over time instead of only snapshots.
- Highlight what is improving and what is slipping.

4) Team scale
- Support more users working in the same workspace.
- Add shared review and approval flows.
- Keep the experience simple even when more people are involved.

5) Higher-level integrations
- Expand webhook and sync options.
- Connect more external systems when needed.
- Keep integration rules predictable and safe.

6) Enterprise controls
- Add more permission layers.
- Support stricter account controls and audit history.
- Keep sensitive actions transparent and logged.

7) Guardrails
- Preserve the done-for-you promise.
- Keep the user in command.
- Never lose the original simplicity of Xspresso.

8) Success criteria
- The system makes better recommendations with less user effort.
- Reporting gives clearer insight.
- Team and integration features work without adding confusion.
- Phase 6 improves power without breaking the simple feel.
