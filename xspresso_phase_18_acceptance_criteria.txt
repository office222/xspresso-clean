Xspresso Phase 18 Acceptance Criteria

This file defines the Phase 18 definition of done.

Acceptance criteria:
- Cross-channel orchestration at scale works as designed.
- AI-assisted campaign optimization suggestions are generated clearly.
- Multi-brand coordination behaves correctly.
- Recommendation loops are useful and actionable.
- Forecasting and trend analysis are available and readable.
- Shared assets and template intelligence can be reused safely.
- Team and agency workflow refinement works smoothly.
- Marketplace / reusable component ideas are represented in the product plan.
- Guardrails and user control remain intact.
- The product remains aligned with Xspresso's core promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, with the user still in command.

Definition of done:
- The feature set is stable enough to support the next expansion phase.
- The user can understand what Xspresso did, what it recommends, and what to do next.
- The system remains simple, safe, and useful.
