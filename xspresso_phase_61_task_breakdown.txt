Xspresso Phase 61 — Task Breakdown

1) Orchestration and workflow refinement
- Tighten the flow across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Keep the main user path fast, clear, and low-friction.

2) Cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- Improve how Xspresso learns from multiple campaigns.
- Surface trends that help the user make better decisions.

3) Recommendation loop improvements
- Make recommendations more specific and more actionable.
- Ensure every recommendation points to a clear next step.

4) Forecasting and performance analysis
- Refine forecasting signals.
- Improve performance summaries so the user can see what is working and what is not.

5) Multi-brand and multi-team workflow refinement
- Support more structured use across brands or teams.
- Keep permissions, ownership, and clarity in place.

6) Shared asset and template marketplace cleanup
- Refine how templates and reusable assets are organized.
- Make it easier to find, reuse, and manage saved work.

7) Dashboard clarity and usability polish
- Improve the readability of dashboard cards, alerts, and usage panels.
- Keep the layout simple and action-focused.

8) Monitoring and alert reliability improvements
- Make alerts consistent and easy to understand.
- Keep underperforming ad warnings and platform notices reliable.

9) Onboarding, help text, and tooltip cleanup
- Polish onboarding prompts and helper text.
- Make tooltips short, useful, and friendly.

10) Release hardening and handoff readiness
- Review the final build for stability.
- Prepare the product for handoff, release, or next-stage packaging.

Success criteria
- The workflow is smooth and easy to follow.
- Recommendations are clear and useful.
- Monitoring and alerts behave consistently.
- Dashboard, help text, and templates are polished.
- The product feels ready for the next release stage.
