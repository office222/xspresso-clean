Xspresso Phase 10 Acceptance Criteria

Purpose:
Define the minimum conditions for Xspresso Phase 10 to be considered complete.

Phase 10 goals:
- Deeper autonomous optimization
- Stronger multi-brand intelligence
- Cross-campaign learning
- Smarter scheduled summaries
- Better recommendation engines
- Forecasting and trends
- Workflow intelligence
- Guardrails and safety

Acceptance criteria:
1. Cross-campaign learning works.
   - The system can identify useful patterns across multiple campaigns.
   - It can suggest improvements based on prior campaign behavior.

2. Deeper autonomous optimization works.
   - The system can propose optimization actions without manual analysis.
   - The user can review, approve, or reject suggestions.

3. Multi-brand intelligence works.
   - The system can separate insights by brand/account.
   - Brand-specific recommendations do not mix across accounts.

4. Scheduled summaries work.
   - The system can generate recurring summaries.
   - Summaries include performance, alerts, trends, and suggested next actions.

5. Recommendation engines work.
   - The system can suggest practical next steps for weak or improving campaigns.
   - Recommendations are understandable and actionable.

6. Forecasting and trend views work.
   - The system can show directional performance trends.
   - Forecasts are clearly marked as estimates, not promises.

7. Workflow intelligence works.
   - The system can help prioritize actions.
   - Suggested tasks are organized by importance.

8. Guardrails remain intact.
   - The user stays in command.
   - The system never changes critical items without confirmation.
   - The system continues to follow originality and safety rules.

9. Reporting remains readable.
   - Insights are concise, useful, and easy to scan.
   - The dashboard continues to show the next best action.

Definition of done:
Xspresso Phase 10 is complete when autonomous optimization, learning, summaries, recommendations, forecasting, and workflow intelligence all function safely and clearly inside the product while keeping the user in control.
