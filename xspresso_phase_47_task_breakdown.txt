Xspresso Phase 47 — Task Breakdown

1) Final refinement and stabilization
- Reduce rough edges in the campaign builder, dashboard, and alert flows.
- Make sure the most common user paths feel fast and obvious.

2) Campaign creation and launch polish
- Clean up the draft, review, approve, and launch sequence.
- Ensure the user always knows what is ready, what is missing, and what happens next.

3) Monitoring and alert tuning
- Improve CPC, CTR, and underperforming-ad warnings.
- Keep alert language short, helpful, and action-oriented.

4) Cross-campaign learning refinement
- Tighten the logic that helps Xspresso learn from user activity and past campaign performance.
- Surface better recommendations over time.

5) Billing and credit clarity
- Make credits, usage, and upgrade prompts easier to understand.
- Keep the fair-usage model visible and simple.

6) Template and library workflow polish
- Improve reuse, duplication, export, import, and restore flows.
- Make it easier to start from saved work.

7) Dashboard clarity improvements
- Make summaries easier to scan.
- Show current status, alerts, and next actions at a glance.

8) Support and help text cleanup
- Tighten tooltips, helper text, and empty states.
- Keep the tone friendly and reassuring.

9) Release readiness and final QA
- Verify the main flows are stable.
- Confirm the product is ready for the next release step.
