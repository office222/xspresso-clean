Xspresso Phase 36 — Task Breakdown

1) Final orchestration polish
- Tighten the flow between campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Make sure each module can pass useful next-step data to the next module.

2) Cross-campaign learning
- Detect patterns across multiple campaigns.
- Reuse winning titles, hooks, angles, and follow-up ideas.
- Surface useful overlaps without copying unrelated content.

3) Recommendation loops
- Turn performance signals into clear suggestions.
- Recommend next actions when ads, campaigns, or leads need attention.
- Keep recommendations short, specific, and actionable.

4) Forecasting and performance analysis
- Estimate likely outcomes from current trends.
- Show easy-to-read summaries for performance direction.
- Highlight changes in CPC, CTR, conversions, and lead quality.

5) Multi-brand / multi-team intelligence
- Support separate brands or workspaces.
- Keep shared logic while respecting account boundaries.
- Let team users see only what they should see.

6) Template marketplace refinement
- Improve shared asset handling.
- Let users reuse their own templates and approved structures.
- Keep the marketplace safe, organized, and easy to search.

7) Workflow coordination
- Coordinate campaign creation, launch, monitoring, alerts, and follow-up.
- Keep the user aware of what happened and what to do next.
- Reduce manual switching between tools and screens.

8) Guardrails and user control
- Keep the user in command.
- Require approvals for sensitive actions.
- Make warnings clear before changes are applied.
- Preserve the "approve what you like, tweak what you want" philosophy.

9) Credit intelligence and usage guidance
- Show how credits are being used.
- Warn when usage is moving toward upgrade territory.
- Suggest better plan fit when it could save money.

10) Dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch-flow polish
- Make the dashboard easier to scan.
- Improve monitoring messages.
- Make alerts more helpful.
- Reduce friction in the launch flow.

Definition of success:
- The product feels coordinated instead of fragmented.
- Recommendations are useful instead of noisy.
- The user still feels in control.
- The system saves time, reduces waste, and supports better decisions.
