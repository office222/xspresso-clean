Xspresso Phase 6 — Acceptance Criteria

Purpose:
Phase 6 is complete when advanced automation, smarter AI guidance, deeper reporting, team scale, higher-level integrations, and enterprise controls work reliably and align with Xspresso’s core promise.

Acceptance Criteria:

1) Advanced automation works
- Users can define automation triggers and outcomes.
- Automation rules execute without manual intervention.
- Failed automations are logged clearly.

2) Smarter AI guidance works
- Xspresso can recommend next best actions based on campaign data.
- Suggestions are relevant, concise, and actionable.
- Guidance improves user decision-making without removing user control.

3) Deeper reporting works
- Users can view detailed campaign, lead, credit, and monitoring reports.
- Reports are readable, useful, and exportable.
- Reporting reflects current data accurately.

4) Team scale works
- Multiple users can collaborate without data conflicts.
- Permissions are respected.
- Shared assets and templates work as expected.

5) Higher-level integrations work
- External services can connect through approved integration paths.
- Webhooks and sync events fire correctly.
- Integration errors are surfaced clearly.

6) Enterprise controls work
- Admin-level controls can be configured safely.
- Audit and activity history remain intact.
- Safety and guardrails remain enforced.

7) Product alignment remains intact
- The product still feels fast, simple, and done-for-you.
- Users still stay in command.
- No feature should weaken the core promise of reducing waste, confusion, and software chaos.

Definition of Done:
- All Phase 6 features function as intended.
- Core workflows remain stable.
- The experience still supports the Xspresso promise: done-for-you marketing, more responses, less software chaos, and the user stays in command.
