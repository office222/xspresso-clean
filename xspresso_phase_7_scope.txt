Xspresso Phase 7 Scope

Phase 7 is for advanced scale and ecosystem growth.

Focus areas:
- multi-brand management
- advanced team workspaces
- shared asset libraries
- deeper analytics and forecasting
- recommendation engine improvements
- workflow automation expansion
- external integrations and connectors
- reporting exports and scheduled summaries
- stronger admin controls
- marketplace / template sharing concepts
- performance insights across multiple campaigns

Phase 7 guardrails:
- keep the core done-for-you promise intact
- do not add complexity that weakens the user experience
- maintain the user-in-command model
- keep credits and usage transparent
- preserve clear alerts and next actions

Success criteria:
- teams can manage multiple brands cleanly
- reporting becomes more actionable
- automation reduces manual work further
- shared resources save time
- the product still feels simple, fast, and guided
