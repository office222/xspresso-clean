Xspresso Phase 45 Scope

This phase covers the final stabilization layer for Xspresso.

Scope:
- final platform and workflow polish
- campaign creation and launch refinement
- monitoring and alert tuning
- cross-campaign learning refinement
- recommendation quality improvements
- billing and credit intelligence refinement
- template and library workflow polish
- dashboard clarity improvements
- support/help text cleanup
- release readiness and final QA pass

Goal:
Make Xspresso feel consistent, reliable, and easy to use across the full workflow.

Success criteria:
- users can build, approve, launch, and monitor campaigns without confusion
- alerts are clear and actionable
- credits and usage are understandable
- platform switching and monitoring rules behave consistently
- library, templates, and duplication flows feel smooth
- dashboard and reports show the right information at a glance
