XSPRESSO BOARD SHELL MASTER HANDOFF

Purpose:
Create a clean, premium, FrontPage-friendly board shell for Xspresso with the logo inline, the cup always left of the name, and the whole header able to shrink gracefully on smaller screens.

Core rules:
- Use Xspresso-only filenames.
- Keep the top header background white.
- Keep the cup logo to the left of the name logo.
- Prevent stacking/wrapping whenever possible.
- Let the logo shrink rather than overlap.
- Keep the business card readable.
- Keep the CTA buttons clear and simple.
- Keep footer/support copy minimal.
- Make the layout feel polished, simple, and premium.

Responsive behavior:
- On smaller screens, the logo should scale down instead of breaking layout.
- The cup and name should stay inline as long as possible.
- If space is tight, the whole header should compress cleanly instead of overlapping.

Suggested board shell structure:
1. White header with cup + name logo
2. Hero / business card component
3. CTA row
4. Short support/footer area

Acceptance checks:
- The cup stays left of the name.
- No logo overlap.
- No ugly wrap/stack issues.
- Buttons are easy to read.
- The board shell looks clean at desktop and mobile widths.

Reminder:
This board shell should reflect the Xspresso promise:
"Done-for-you marketing. You stay in command. We do the work."
