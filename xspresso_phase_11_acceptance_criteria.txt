Xspresso Phase 11 — Acceptance Criteria

- Autonomous optimization loops are functional and safe.
- Multi-brand intelligence works across separate brand profiles.
- Cross-campaign learning improves recommendations over time.
- Scheduled summaries are generated on time and are readable.
- Recommendation engines produce useful next-step guidance.
- Forecasting and trend detection are available and understandable.
- Workflow intelligence improves user actions without removing control.
- Guardrails and safety checks prevent unsafe or unwanted actions.
- The system remains aligned with the core Xspresso promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, with the user still in command.
- Phase 11 is complete when the above items work reliably and can be reviewed without confusion.
