Xspresso Phase 55 — Task Breakdown

1) Final release stabilization
- Review all core V1 flows end to end
- Confirm campaign creation, edit, launch, and monitoring work cleanly
- Verify no major blockers remain

2) Monitoring and alerts polish
- Tune alert thresholds
- Confirm underperforming ad warnings are clear
- Verify regenerate recommendations appear at the right time
- Check platform switch warnings and monitoring behavior

3) Billing and credits clarity
- Validate plan limits and credit usage math
- Confirm a la carte actions charge correctly
- Check upgrade suggestion messaging
- Review renewal and low-credit reminders

4) Template, library, and reuse workflow
- Verify save, duplicate, restore, and export flows
- Confirm user-owned reuse behavior is clear
- Check version history and undo behavior

5) Dashboard and reporting polish
- Review summary cards, campaign cards, and alert feed
- Confirm activity logs and usage history are easy to read
- Make sure every screen suggests the next action

6) Help text, onboarding, and tooltips
- Clean up onboarding copy
- Improve helper text for monitoring, credits, and switching platforms
- Make warning language friendly and direct

7) Final QA and handoff readiness
- Test for broken links, broken states, or unclear flows
- Confirm the product feels simple, fast, and done-for-you
- Prepare for release or final build handoff

Success criteria
- Core V1 feels polished and stable
- Users can build, launch, monitor, improve, and reuse campaigns
- Credits, alerts, billing, and switching rules are understandable
- The system keeps the user in command while Xspresso does the work
