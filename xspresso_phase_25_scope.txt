Xspresso Phase 25 Scope

Focus:
- deeper autonomous orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- stronger recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and usage intelligence
- improved multi-brand / multi-team coordination
- more refined workflow automation
- richer template / asset marketplace ideas
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance

Success criteria:
- the system coordinates more of the workflow with less manual work
- recommendations become more accurate and more timely
- users still stay in command and can approve, tweak, or override anything
- all tools remain available with fair, transparent usage rules
