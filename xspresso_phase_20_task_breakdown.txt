Xspresso Phase 20 — Task Breakdown

1) Deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- Connect the major product modules so they share state cleanly.
- Ensure campaign changes flow into leads, alerts, billing, and reporting.
- Keep the system responsive and consistent across screens.

2) Smarter cross-campaign learning
- Detect patterns across multiple campaigns.
- Reuse helpful learnings to improve new outputs.
- Surface shared insights without overwhelming the user.

3) More advanced recommendation loops
- Recommend the next best action when a campaign or ad needs attention.
- Prioritize recommendations by likely impact.
- Offer one-click fixes when possible.

4) Broader forecasting and trend analysis
- Show trend direction for performance, credits, and usage.
- Give simple future-facing indicators that help users plan.
- Keep forecasting clear and easy to scan.

5) Shared asset and template intelligence
- Reuse high-performing templates and assets.
- Make templates easier to search, organize, and apply.
- Support reusable building blocks across campaigns.

6) Stronger team and agency controls
- Add roles, permissions, and accountability.
- Support multiple users working on shared accounts.
- Keep ownership and access rules clear.

7) Marketplace / reusable component ideas
- Prepare for sharing templates and assets.
- Let users reuse approved components across accounts.
- Keep any sharing tied to user-owned work.

8) Tighter guardrails and user control
- Keep the user in command.
- Make all important actions reviewable, editable, and reversible.
- Maintain clear warnings, confirmations, and activity history.

9) Success criteria
- Orchestration works reliably.
- Learning loops improve recommendations.
- Forecasting is understandable.
- Shared assets remain organized.
- Team controls stay safe.
- The product still feels fast, simple, and done-for-you.
