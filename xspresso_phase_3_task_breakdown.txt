Xspresso Phase 3 Task Breakdown

Purpose:
Phase 3 focuses on billing, credits, monitoring polish, dashboard refinement, platform switching clarity, and overall stability.

Phase 3 Tasks:
1. Billing and Subscription Refinement
- Confirm plan tiers
- Confirm renewal timing
- Confirm upgrade/downgrade behavior
- Confirm pause/cancel/reactivate flows
- Confirm renewal reminders

2. Credit and Usage Controls
- Finalize credit display across the app
- Finalize credit consumption rules
- Finalize a la carte pricing actions
- Show credits used, remaining, and tied to monitoring
- Add low-credit warnings

3. Monitoring and Alert Polish
- Refine underperforming ad alerts
- Refine CPC and CTR warning thresholds
- Improve recommendation text
- Make monitoring messages short and actionable
- Ensure every alert suggests a next step

4. Platform Switching Improvements
- Finalize switch warning modal
- Show ad-by-ad decisions clearly
- Confirm delete-to-library wording
- Finalize daily / weekly / monthly monitoring options
- Add rush vs standard handling

5. Dashboard Improvements
- Make campaign cards easier to scan
- Show clear status badges
- Show alerts and next actions at a glance
- Show credits remaining and usage summary
- Keep the dashboard simple and actionable

6. Library and Reuse
- Confirm save, duplicate, export, and import flows
- Keep version history intact
- Keep user-owned campaigns reusable
- Ensure restore flows are clear

7. Stability and Polish
- Fix edge cases
- Improve empty states
- Improve error messages
- Check accessibility and readability
- Reduce clutter across the app

Phase 3 Guardrails:
- Keep all tools available on all plans
- Credits determine usage, not access
- Keep the user in command
- Keep copy short, clear, and helpful
- Always recommend a next action

Success Criteria:
- Billing feels simple and fair
- Credit usage is visible and understandable
- Monitoring alerts are useful and actionable
- Platform switching is clear and controlled
- Dashboard feels easy to scan
- The product feels stable and ready for broader use
