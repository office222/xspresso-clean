Xspresso Safety and Guardrails

Purpose:
Keep Xspresso helpful, original, user-controlled, and safe to use.

Core rules:
- Never copy another person's work.
- Use inspiration and strategy only.
- Let users approve, tweak, or reject outputs.
- Keep users in control of all launches and changes.
- Show clear warnings before tracking stops, switches, or usage charges.
- Be transparent about credits, monitoring, and a la carte usage.
- Do not hide feature limits or surprise users with missing tools.
- Give helpful recommendations, not guarantees.
- Make it clear when Xspresso is monitoring, regenerating, exporting, or billing usage.

Copy principles:
- Friendly, clear, and direct.
- No hype that overpromises results.
- No confusing jargon when simple wording works better.
- Emphasize done-for-you, but still user-controlled.

Monitoring principles:
- Alerts should explain the issue and recommend the next action.
- Underperforming ads should prompt regeneration or improvement.
- Switching platforms should always show a clear warning.
- Users should know exactly when credits are being spent.

Privacy and consent:
- Only use customer data for approved campaign actions.
- Require explicit consent for tracking, monitoring, and third-party access where needed.
- Keep user data protected and auditable.
- Make it easy to review, export, or remove user-owned content.

Product promise:
Xspresso helps users move faster, waste less, and stay in command while the system does the heavy lifting.
