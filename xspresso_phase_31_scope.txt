Xspresso Phase 31 — Scope

This phase continues the long-term expansion of Xspresso with a focus on final refinement, orchestration, and product intelligence.

Scope includes:
- deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- stronger recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- shared assets and template marketplace refinement
- improved workflow coordination
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- continued polish for dashboard, monitoring, and alerts

Success criteria:
- Xspresso stays simple for the user
- the system remains useful, fast, and clear
- recommendations help users save time, money, and effort
- the product continues to feel done-for-you, while the user stays in command
