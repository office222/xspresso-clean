Xspresso Phase 19 Acceptance Criteria

This file defines when Phase 19 is considered complete.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Campaigns can be orchestrated across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Cross-campaign learning improves recommendations.
- Recommendation loops provide useful next actions.
- Forecasting and trend analysis are visible and understandable.
- Shared assets and template intelligence work reliably.
- Team and agency controls are enforced correctly.
- Marketplace / reusable component ideas can be supported.
- Guardrails and user control remain intact.
- The system stays simple enough for users to understand.
- The phase can be considered complete when these capabilities are stable and usable together.

Definition of Done:
- Users can create, monitor, optimize, and manage assets with minimal friction.
- The system can surface useful recommendations without confusing the user.
- Guardrails prevent unsafe or unsupported actions.
- All major workflows related to Phase 19 operate consistently.
