Xspresso Phase 6 Scope

Purpose:
Phase 6 is the advanced expansion phase for Xspresso after V1 stability and the core workflow have been proven. The goal of Phase 6 is to deepen automation, improve team scale, and add higher-value options for power users and agencies.

Phase 6 goals:
- Expand advanced automation
- Add smarter AI guidance and suggestions
- Improve reporting depth and insights
- Support more robust team workflows
- Add higher-level integrations
- Improve shared assets and collaboration
- Introduce more enterprise-ready controls
- Prepare the platform for larger customers and more complex usage patterns

Possible Phase 6 feature areas:
- Advanced AI assistant prompts and recommendations
- Multi-step workflow automation
- Deeper campaign optimization suggestions
- More advanced reporting views
- Scheduled summaries and alerts
- Shared campaign workspaces
- Advanced permission controls
- API expansions and webhook options
- Additional template and asset sharing tools
- More flexible library management
- Optional white-label or agency-facing controls
- Expanded analytics for leads, ads, and monitoring

Guardrails:
- Keep all changes aligned with the core Xspresso promise
- Do not make the product harder to use for new users
- Preserve the done-for-you feel
- Keep the user in command
- Avoid unnecessary feature bloat
- Maintain credits / usage fairness
- Keep all tools visible and understandable

Success criteria:
- Phase 6 should feel like a natural expansion, not a rewrite
- Advanced users should gain more power without losing simplicity
- Agencies should be able to manage more work with less friction
- Reporting and automation should become more useful and more predictive
- The product should remain fast, simple, and customer-focused

Notes:
Phase 6 should only begin after Phase 1 through Phase 5 are stable, documented, and usable.
