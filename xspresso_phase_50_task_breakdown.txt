Xspresso Phase 50 — Task Breakdown

1) Final product consolidation
- Review all V1 modules and make sure they work together cleanly.
- Remove redundant flows and simplify handoff points.
- Confirm the product still matches the core promise.

2) Release readiness
- Verify the app is stable enough for release.
- Check that campaign creation, editing, launch, monitoring, and alerts all connect properly.
- Make sure the user can move through the app without dead ends.

3) Core V1 feature finalization
- Finalize landing page generation.
- Finalize ad creation.
- Finalize follow-up automation.
- Finalize platform adaptation.
- Finalize campaign library and duplication.

4) Monitoring and alerts
- Finalize performance checks.
- Finalize underperforming ad warnings.
- Finalize CPC and CTR alerts.
- Finalize platform switch warnings and monitoring choices.

5) Credits, billing, and usage behavior
- Finalize credits usage rules.
- Finalize a la carte usage items.
- Finalize monitoring costs.
- Finalize upgrade suggestions when usage grows.

6) Platform switching rules
- Finalize the warning modal.
- Finalize per-ad and bulk choices.
- Finalize delete-to-library wording.
- Finalize keep-monitoring pricing.

7) Dashboard and reporting
- Finalize the dashboard summary cards.
- Finalize campaign card actions.
- Finalize alert center behavior.
- Finalize activity history and usage reporting.

8) Onboarding, help, and support
- Finalize onboarding questions.
- Finalize tooltips and inline help.
- Finalize FAQ and objection handling.
- Finalize empty states and error messages.

9) Quality assurance
- Check each screen flow from login to launch.
- Confirm guardrails are in place.
- Confirm copy is clear and consistent.
- Confirm the product still feels done-for-you but user-controlled.

10) Success criteria
- The product is ready to ship as a polished V1.
- The user can build, edit, launch, monitor, and improve campaigns.
- Credits, billing, and usage rules are clear.
- Warnings, alerts, and recommendations are helpful.
- The core promise remains simple: done-for-you marketing, fast.
