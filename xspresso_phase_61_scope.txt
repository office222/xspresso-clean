Xspresso Phase 61 Scope

This phase continues final stabilization and handoff readiness for Xspresso.

Covers:
- deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- stronger recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team workflow refinement
- shared asset and template marketplace cleanup
- dashboard clarity and usability polish
- monitoring and alert reliability improvements
- onboarding, help text, and tooltip cleanup
- release hardening and handoff readiness

Primary goals:
- keep the product simple for users
- keep the user in command
- avoid feature clutter
- make warnings, credits, and monitoring clearer
- improve trust, visibility, and workflow smoothness

Acceptance ideas:
- campaigns create, edit, approve, launch, and monitor cleanly
- alerts are understandable and actionable
- credits and billing are easy to read
- platform switching warnings are clear
- templates, library, and reuse workflows are smooth
- dashboard shows what matters at a glance
