Xspresso Component Map

Purpose:
A simple reference for the main pieces of Xspresso V1 so the build stays organized.

Core areas:
1. Campaign Builder
- Add product photo, URL, description, audience, goal, and tone.
- Generate headlines, hooks, ads, landing page copy, cart copy, lead magnet copy, and follow-up emails.

2. Review and Edit
- Approve sections individually.
- Edit one section without rebuilding the rest.
- Regenerate one section or the whole campaign.

3. Launch Flow
- Check readiness before launch.
- Show what is missing.
- Confirm go-live.

4. Monitoring Dashboard
- Show active campaigns, monitored ads, credits, and alerts.
- Warn when CPC rises or performance drops.
- Recommend the next best action.

5. Platform Switching
- Warn the user when switching platforms.
- Offer keep running, move to library, or keep monitoring for a usage cost.
- Support per-ad or bulk choices.

6. Credits and Usage
- All plans include every tool.
- Credits determine fair usage.
- Usage can be tracked by action, monitoring, uploads, or extra items.

7. Lead Capture and Follow-Up
- Capture leads.
- Score leads.
- Route leads to the right follow-up sequence.
- Keep the conversation going automatically.

8. Library and Reuse
- Save campaigns.
- Duplicate user-owned work.
- Export and import templates.
- Reuse winning campaigns without starting from scratch.

9. Alerts and Notifications
- Notify users when ads underperform.
- Recommend regeneration before more credits are spent.
- Keep messages short, useful, and clear.

10. Settings and Controls
- Let the user choose tone, thresholds, defaults, and platform behavior.
- Keep the user in command.

Core product promise:
Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
