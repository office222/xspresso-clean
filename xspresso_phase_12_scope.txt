Xspresso Phase 12 — Scope

This phase is focused on final polish, hardening, and operational readiness after the core AI optimization system is in place.

Includes:
- final UX polish across the app
- performance hardening and stability work
- deeper error handling and recovery flows
- stronger onboarding and help content
- improved dashboard clarity and summaries
- better audit trails and activity visibility
- more polished billing/credits presentation
- support tooling and troubleshooting aids
- final platform compatibility checks
- release candidate preparation

Success criteria:
- app feels stable and reliable in daily use
- users can complete the full workflow with minimal confusion
- alerts, monitoring, and billing behave consistently
- support and help content reduce friction
- the product is ready for broader public launch

Guardrails:
- keep the product aligned with done-for-you marketing
- preserve user control and approval
- keep all tools available to all plans
- preserve the credits-based fair usage model
- avoid unnecessary feature creep
