Xspresso Phase 21 — Acceptance Criteria

This file defines the acceptance criteria for the next major stage of Xspresso.

1) Autonomous orchestration
- The system can coordinate campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports with less manual intervention.
- Workflow steps are connected cleanly across modules.

2) Cross-campaign learning
- The system can use performance from one campaign to inform suggestions on another.
- Recommendations should be consistent and explainable.

3) Recommendation loops
- The system should suggest the next best action when performance changes.
- Suggestions should be clear, actionable, and user-friendly.

4) Forecasting and trend analysis
- The system should surface trends over time.
- Forecast summaries should be understandable at a glance.

5) Multi-brand intelligence
- The system should support smarter management across multiple brands, campaigns, or workspaces.
- Separate workspaces should stay organized and distinct.

6) Shared assets and template intelligence
- Reusable templates and assets should remain easy to find and apply.
- Shared components should not break the user’s flow.

7) Team and agency controls
- Team permissions should remain enforced.
- Shared work should still respect user roles and access limits.

8) Guardrails and user control
- The user must remain in command.
- Regeneration, approval, and launch actions must remain explicit.
- The system must not act silently on major decisions.

9) Credit intelligence
- Usage suggestions should be clear when credits are being consumed heavily.
- The system should surface cost-saving suggestions when appropriate.

10) Definition of done
- The phase is complete when orchestration, learning, recommendations, forecasting, and user control all work together without making the product confusing or unstable.
