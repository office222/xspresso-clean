Xspresso Board Shell — Release Ready Notes

Goal:
Keep the board shell clean, premium, readable, and consistent with the Xspresso brand.

Must-haves:
- White header background
- Cup logo stays left of the name logo
- Logo scales down cleanly on smaller screens
- No wrapping or stacking issues unless the screen is extremely narrow
- Business card content stays readable
- CTA buttons remain clear and easy to click
- Footer remains simple and uncluttered

Content focus:
- Done-for-you marketing
- You stay in command
- We do the work
- More responses
- Less software chaos

Layout reminders:
- Use orange as an accent, not the dominant background
- Keep spacing consistent between sections
- Keep the hero clean and short
- Keep action buttons near the main message
- Avoid repeating the same message too many times

Acceptance check:
- Looks good on a wide screen
- Still readable when the page shrinks
- Logo remains inline
- No duplicate filenames
- FrontPage-friendly structure

Next step:
Create the final release bundle once the board shell is visually approved.
