Xspresso Board Shell Implementation Checklist

Purpose:
A quick checklist to help finalize the board shell and keep the header, business card, CTA, footer, and responsive behavior consistent.

Checklist:
- Keep the cup logo left of the Xspresso name.
- Use a white header background.
- Make sure the logo shrinks cleanly on smaller screens.
- Prevent the logo from stacking or wrapping awkwardly.
- Keep the business card component readable and balanced.
- Keep the main message short and premium.
- Use CTA buttons with clear hierarchy.
- Keep the primary CTA more prominent than the secondary CTA.
- Keep footer support links simple and useful.
- Keep all copy aligned with the core promise: done-for-you marketing, fast.
- Maintain the voice: more responses, less software chaos, you stay in command.
- Confirm the layout still looks good in FrontPage-style editing.

Acceptance checks:
- Header stays inline.
- Card remains readable.
- Buttons are easy to scan.
- Footer does not feel crowded.
- Page feels polished, simple, and premium.
