Xspresso Phase 32 — Acceptance Criteria

This file defines the acceptance criteria for Phase 32 of Xspresso.

Phase 32 is complete when:
- deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports is stable
- smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection are working
- stronger recommendation loops are producing useful next-step guidance
- broader forecasting and performance analysis is available
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence works cleanly
- shared assets and template marketplace refinement is supported
- improved workflow coordination is reliable
- tighter guardrails and user control are enforced
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance is visible to users
- dashboard, monitoring, and alerts remain polished and usable

Definition of done:
- the Phase 32 workflow is consistent, testable, and ready for the next expansion layer
- users can understand what Xspresso is doing, what it costs, and what to do next
- Xspresso continues to feel done-for-you, fast, simple, and under user control
