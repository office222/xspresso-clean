Xspresso Phase 14 Scope

Purpose:
This phase represents the long-range expansion layer after the core Xspresso system is stable.

Focus areas:
- deeper autonomous optimization
- broader multi-brand intelligence
- cross-campaign learning across accounts and offers
- more advanced recommendation loops
- richer forecasting and trend analysis
- smarter workflow orchestration
- stronger team and agency controls
- improved marketplace / template sharing ideas
- additional integrations and automations
- continued safety, permissions, and guardrails

Guiding principle:
Keep the system simple for the user while making the backend smarter over time.

Success criteria:
- the system continues to reduce manual work
- recommendations become more useful over time
- multi-brand and multi-team usage stays organized
- the product remains fast, clear, and easy to command
