Xspresso Phase 48 — Acceptance Criteria

Phase 48 is complete when the following are true:

- Campaign creation, editing, approval, and launch flows remain stable.
- Monitoring, alerting, and ad regeneration continue to work consistently.
- Billing, credits, and usage reporting remain clear and accurate.
- Platform switching behavior remains predictable and user-friendly.
- Library, templates, export/import, and duplicate workflows function properly.
- Dashboard views continue to show useful campaign, alert, and usage summaries.
- Recommendation logic continues to suggest useful next actions.
- Cross-campaign learning and trend detection remain available and stable.
- Multi-brand and multi-team coordination remains intact.
- Guardrails, user control, and safety rules continue to be enforced.
- Support/help text and empty states remain clear and helpful.
- Final release polish is maintained across the app.

Definition of done:
The Phase 48 layer is complete when Xspresso continues to feel reliable, understandable, and easy to use across campaign creation, monitoring, billing, and optimization workflows.
