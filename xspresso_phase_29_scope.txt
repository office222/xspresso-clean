Xspresso Phase 29 — Scope

This phase covers the next refinement layer for Xspresso.

Includes:
- deeper orchestration and optimization
- stronger cross-campaign learning
- smarter recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and trend analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence
- shared asset and template marketplace refinement
- tighter guardrails and user control
- improved credit intelligence and usage guidance

Success criteria:
- Xspresso continues to reduce manual work
- recommendations remain actionable and easy to understand
- campaigns, leads, billing, and alerts stay coordinated
- the user stays in command while Xspresso does more of the heavy lifting
