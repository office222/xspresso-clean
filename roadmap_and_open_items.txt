Xspresso — Roadmap and Open Items

Current direction:
- Done-for-you marketing system
- Fast, simple, all-in-one workflow
- User stays in command while Xspresso does the heavy lifting
- Credits determine fair usage

Completed build areas:
- Campaign generation
- Ad monitoring and alerts
- Lead capture and follow-up
- Billing and credits
- Platform switching rules
- Library / reuse / duplication
- Templates and presets
- Dashboard and campaign cards
- Notification system
- Security and permissions
- Integration planning
- Test plan

Open items to refine next:
1. Video production flow
   - turn campaigns into short videos and VSL assets
   - define editable sections and export options

2. Social upload workflow
   - per-platform formatting and upload rules
   - direct upload limits per plan
   - one-click publish / schedule options

3. Reporting and analytics
   - trend charts
   - campaign performance summaries
   - credits used vs results gained

4. AI assistant prompts
   - smarter suggestions for headlines, hooks, and offers
   - improved regeneration prompts for underperforming ads

5. V2 feature candidates
   - sales letters
   - VSL scripts
   - video production
   - cloning of own campaigns only
   - advanced automation and optimization

Working principles:
- Never copy someone else’s work
- Allow users to use their own campaigns as templates
- Always show clear warnings before actions that affect live ads
- Recommend fixes before spending more credits on underperforming ads
- Keep every plan useful by including every tool

Next recommended build block:
- Video production workflow
