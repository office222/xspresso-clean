Xspresso Phase 3 Acceptance Criteria

Phase 3 is complete when the following are true:

1. Billing and usage are clear
- Users can see credits remaining, credits used, and credits tied to monitoring.
- Users can understand when and why credits are spent.
- Upgrade suggestions appear only when they are likely to save money.

2. Monitoring is stable and useful
- Ads can be tracked reliably.
- Underperforming ads trigger clear warnings.
- Alerts recommend a next action.
- Regeneration is available for weak ads.

3. Platform switching is understandable
- Switching off a platform shows a clear warning.
- Users can keep specific ads running.
- Users can move ads to their library.
- Users can keep monitoring for a usage cost.
- Daily, weekly, and monthly monitoring terms are visible.

4. Dashboard is informative
- The dashboard shows active campaigns, alerts, monitored ads, and credits remaining.
- Campaign cards show status, performance, and quick actions.
- Users can see what needs attention quickly.

5. Library and reuse work well
- Users can save campaigns to the library.
- Users can duplicate their own work.
- Users can export and import their own campaigns.
- Users can restore previous versions.

6. Stability and polish are acceptable
- The app does not feel broken or confusing.
- Core flows are fast and understandable.
- Errors are clear.
- Empty states are helpful.
- The system feels ready for real use.

7. Product promise still holds
- Xspresso remains done-for-you.
- The user stays in command.
- All tools remain included.
- Credits determine usage and fair use.
- The experience still feels fast and simple.

Definition of done:
If a user can create a campaign, edit it, launch it, monitor it, respond to alerts, manage credits, and reuse their work without confusion, Phase 3 is successful.
