Xspresso Board Shell Typography and Spacing Notes

Purpose:
Keep the board shell clean, premium, readable, and responsive.

Typography rules:
- Use one clear headline style.
- Use one supporting style for short benefit lines.
- Keep body copy short and scannable.
- Avoid too many font sizes on the same screen.
- Use bold only for key promise lines, CTA labels, and short emphasis.

Spacing rules:
- Give the logo/header room to breathe.
- Use consistent padding around the business card component.
- Keep the CTA area separated from the main copy.
- Use even spacing between bullet points or benefit lines.
- Avoid large empty gaps that break the flow.
- Avoid tight stacking that makes the shell feel crowded.

Layout rules:
- Keep the cup left of the name.
- Keep the header background white.
- Keep the business card readable at smaller widths.
- Let the layout shrink without stacking the logo awkwardly.
- Keep buttons aligned and easy to scan.

Copy tone:
- Clear
- Simple
- Premium
- Direct
- Customer-focused

Best product rule:
The board shell should feel easy to read, easy to trust, and easy to act on.

Acceptance checks:
- Logo stays inline.
- Text remains readable when the page shrinks.
- Spacing feels even.
- CTA buttons remain obvious.
- The overall shell feels polished, not crowded.
