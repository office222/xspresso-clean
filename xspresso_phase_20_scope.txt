Xspresso Phase 20 — Scope

Purpose:
Phase 20 focuses on the next expansion layer for Xspresso: deeper orchestration, stronger cross-campaign intelligence, richer recommendations, and more automated workflow coordination while keeping the product simple and user-controlled.

Scope:
- Deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- Smarter cross-campaign learning
- More advanced recommendation loops
- Broader forecasting and trend analysis
- Shared asset and template intelligence
- Stronger team and agency controls
- Marketplace / reusable component ideas
- Tighter guardrails and user control

Principles:
- Keep Xspresso done-for-you
- Keep the user in command
- Never copy; always create original outputs
- Make the next best action obvious
- Reduce waste, time, and tool juggling

Success Criteria:
- Xspresso can coordinate more of the workflow automatically
- Users still feel in control
- Recommendations are useful, timely, and specific
- The system stays simple enough to use
