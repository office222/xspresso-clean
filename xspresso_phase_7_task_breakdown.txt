Xspresso Phase 7 — Task Breakdown

1) Multi-brand management
- Support multiple brands inside one account.
- Let users switch between brands quickly.
- Keep separate campaigns, assets, and reports per brand.

2) Advanced team workspaces
- Add team workspaces for separate groups.
- Allow shared access by workspace.
- Keep campaign ownership and visibility clear.

3) Shared asset libraries
- Create shared libraries for ads, images, hooks, headlines, and templates.
- Allow users to reuse approved assets across campaigns.
- Keep version history and source attribution.

4) Deeper analytics and forecasting
- Add trend tracking over time.
- Show forecasted performance where possible.
- Compare campaign results across brands and workspaces.

5) Workflow automation expansion
- Expand rule-based automations.
- Let actions trigger on alerts, performance changes, or lead activity.
- Support more advanced sequences and branching.

6) Integrations and scheduled summaries
- Add more third-party integrations.
- Support scheduled email or dashboard summaries.
- Allow users to choose what they want to receive and when.

7) Marketplace / template sharing
- Let users share approved templates.
- Support a marketplace-style template library.
- Keep sharing focused on user-owned or approved assets.

8) Guardrails
- Keep originality rules in place.
- Preserve user control and approval.
- Protect credits, permissions, and data access.
- Keep the "never copy" rule intact.

9) Success criteria
- Users can manage multiple brands cleanly.
- Teams can work in shared spaces.
- Shared assets remain organized.
- Analytics are more useful.
- Automations save time.
- Sharing stays safe and controlled.

10) Phase 7 definition of done
- Multi-brand support works.
- Team workspaces work.
- Shared libraries work.
- Forecasting/reporting works.
- Automation and integrations work.
- Sharing remains safe and approved.
