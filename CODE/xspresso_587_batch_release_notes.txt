Xspresso batch release notes
===========================

This batch adds the next app foundation:
- dashboard metric helpers
- monitoring and ad health evaluation
- alerts and notification helpers
- lead capture, scoring, and follow-up timeline
- settings store and defaults

Recommended usage:
- keep these files in the app/logic layer
- connect dashboard widgets to the dashboard metrics helpers
- connect alerts screens to alerts center helpers
- connect lead capture UI to the lead timeline helpers
- connect settings screens to the settings store helpers

Core product reminder:
Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
We do the work. You enjoy the benefits.
