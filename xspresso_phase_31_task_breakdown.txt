Xspresso Phase 31 — Task Breakdown

1) Campaign orchestration
- Connect the campaign builder to the monitoring and alert system.
- Make sure campaign states stay in sync across draft, live, paused, and underperforming.
- Keep launch, edit, regenerate, and duplicate actions available from the dashboard.

2) Cross-campaign learning
- Feed performance data back into future recommendations.
- Surface patterns across campaigns, leads, alerts, and billing.
- Suggest better hooks, titles, angles, and follow-up paths based on prior results.

3) Recommendation engine
- Recommend next actions when ads are underperforming.
- Suggest when to regenerate, pause, or tweak.
- Prioritize actions that protect credits and reduce wasted spend.

4) Multi-brand and multi-team support
- Support separate workspaces for different brands or client accounts.
- Keep permissions and shared assets organized.
- Make sure templates, libraries, and reports stay scoped correctly.

5) Shared asset and template refinement
- Improve how templates are saved, duplicated, and reused.
- Allow users to start from proven campaign structures.
- Keep the library simple to browse and restore from.

6) Credit intelligence and usage guidance
- Show smarter warnings when usage is rising.
- Suggest the best plan if the user is repeatedly hitting usage limits.
- Make daily, weekly, and monthly monitoring charges easy to understand.

7) Guardrails and user control
- Keep the user in command.
- Require clear confirmations for major actions.
- Make every important switch, alert, and regeneration understandable.

8) Success criteria
- Users can run campaigns without confusion.
- Xspresso can guide the user to better actions.
- Credits, monitoring, and alerts are easy to understand.
- The system feels done-for-you without removing control.
