Xspresso Phase 28 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- The final refinement layer works smoothly
- Deeper orchestration and optimization behave correctly
- Cross-campaign learning improves recommendations
- Recommendation loops are accurate and useful
- Forecasting and trend analysis produce actionable summaries
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence stays consistent
- Shared asset and template marketplace refinement is functional
- Guardrails and user control remain intact
- Credit intelligence and usage guidance are clear
- The system remains simple, stable, and aligned with the Xspresso promise

Definition of done:
Xspresso can coordinate campaigns, improve recommendations, and guide users clearly while staying fast, simple, and done-for-you.
