Xspresso Phase 8 — Task Breakdown

1) Smarter optimization suggestions
- Detect patterns in low performance.
- Recommend the most likely next improvement.
- Rank suggestions by expected impact.
- Surface one-click actions for the best fix.

2) Deeper performance analysis
- Show trend lines for CPC, CTR, conversions, and spend.
- Compare current performance against prior periods.
- Highlight strong and weak campaigns.
- Summarize what changed and why.

3) More adaptive campaign recommendations
- Suggest new titles, hooks, and angles based on results.
- Recommend refreshes when a campaign stalls.
- Offer alternate versions by platform.
- Adapt recommendations based on campaign type.

4) Stronger team and workspace controls
- Shared workspaces for teams.
- Role-based permissions.
- Approval routing.
- Activity visibility by user and workspace.

5) Scheduled reporting
- Daily summaries.
- Weekly summaries.
- Monthly summaries.
- Automated report delivery by email or dashboard.

6) Advanced automation rules
- Trigger changes when thresholds are crossed.
- Auto-flag poor performers.
- Auto-suggest regeneration.
- Auto-route leads and follow-up tasks.

7) Better account insights
- Show credit usage patterns.
- Highlight the most expensive actions.
- Show which campaigns use the most monitoring.
- Recommend ways to save credits.

8) Forecasting ideas
- Estimate future credit usage.
- Estimate likely campaign need based on current activity.
- Predict when a user may need to upgrade.
- Predict when a campaign may need a refresh.

9) Clearer credit intelligence
- Explain why credits were spent.
- Show the value of each action.
- Warn before waste.
- Suggest the most efficient next move.

10) Success criteria
- Users receive better recommendations.
- Reports are easier to understand.
- Teams can work more cleanly.
- Credits are more transparent.
- The system feels smarter and more helpful.
