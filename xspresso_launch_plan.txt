Xspresso Launch Plan

Purpose:
This file defines the recommended launch process for Xspresso, from final review to public release, with an emphasis on keeping the product simple, done-for-you, and easy to understand.

Core launch message:
Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
We do the work. You enjoy the benefits.

Launch phases:

1) Pre-Launch Review
- Confirm the core promise is consistent across the hero, feature section, pricing, and founder story.
- Confirm V1 scope is clear.
- Confirm the app, pricing, and credits system all match the same rules.
- Confirm platform switch warnings and monitoring behavior are understandable.
- Confirm all copy is customer-focused and easy to scan.

2) Soft Launch
- Release to a small group of users first.
- Watch where users hesitate or stop.
- Track which sections are confusing.
- Track which actions are used most often.
- Track where users spend credits fastest.
- Collect feedback on onboarding, campaign generation, and monitoring.

3) Public Launch
- Open the product to all users.
- Keep the messaging simple.
- Keep the hero focused on done-for-you marketing.
- Keep the pricing model clear: all tools included, credits determine usage.
- Keep the dashboard easy to understand.
- Keep alerts short and helpful.

4) Post-Launch Optimization
- Review usage logs.
- Review alerts.
- Review underperforming ad fixes.
- Review platform switch behavior.
- Review credit usage patterns.
- Improve confusing screens.
- Add better defaults where needed.
- Refine the onboarding flow.

Launch checklist:
- Hero section approved
- Feature list approved
- Pricing table approved
- Founder story approved
- Dashboard approved
- Monitoring rules approved
- Credits and a la carte rules approved
- Platform switch modal approved
- Alerts and warnings approved
- Support/help copy approved
- V1 scope locked

Suggested launch priority:
- Campaign builder
- Draft generation
- Review and approve flow
- Launch flow
- Monitoring dashboard
- Alerts and regeneration
- Lead capture and follow-up
- Credits and subscription logic
- Platform switching rules
- Library and reuse tools

Launch success indicators:
- Users understand what Xspresso does quickly
- Users can build a campaign without confusion
- Users can review and tweak without starting over
- Users can launch without needing help
- Users understand their credit usage
- Users understand when to regenerate an underperforming ad
- Users understand what happens when they switch platforms

Public-facing launch language:
- Done-for-you marketing, fast.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.
- All plans include every tool. Credits simply determine fair usage.
- Improve before spending more credits on something that is not performing.

Final launch rule:
Do not launch with unclear pricing, unclear monitoring rules, or unclear credits. The first launch should feel simple, helpful, and trustworthy.
