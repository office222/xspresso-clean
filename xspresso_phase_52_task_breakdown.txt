Xspresso Phase 52 — Task Breakdown

1) Final stabilization and polish
- Clean up any remaining rough edges in the core V1 flow.
- Improve consistency across the builder, dashboard, alerts, and library.

2) End-to-end campaign flow cleanup
- Confirm the full flow works from offer input to draft generation to launch.
- Make sure review, edit, approve, and regenerate actions stay simple.

3) Monitoring and alerts refinement
- Tighten alert wording for underperforming ads, rising CPC, and paused tracking.
- Make sure alerts always suggest a next action.

4) Billing and credit clarity
- Keep plan usage and credit consumption easy to understand.
- Make sure usage, a la carte, and renewal messaging stays clear.

5) Templates, library, and reuse workflow polish
- Confirm users can save, duplicate, restore, and reuse their own campaigns.
- Keep template and library actions obvious and low-friction.

6) Onboarding and help text cleanup
- Polish onboarding steps, tooltips, and support text.
- Make sure users always know what to do next.

7) Dashboard clarity improvements
- Keep the dashboard simple, readable, and action-focused.
- Show campaign status, alerts, credits, and next steps at a glance.

8) Support packaging and release readiness
- Prepare the product for handoff, QA, and release.
- Make sure the V1 build is stable, understandable, and ready to ship.
