Xspresso Phase 20 — Acceptance Criteria

Goal:
Validate the final Phase 20 scope for deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.

Acceptance Criteria:

1. Campaign Orchestration
- The system can coordinate campaigns across multiple modules.
- Campaign updates flow correctly between builder, monitor, alerts, and reporting.
- Cross-campaign learning is reflected in recommendations.

2. Recommendation Engine
- Xspresso can suggest next actions based on campaign and ad performance.
- Recommendations are clear, actionable, and user-friendly.
- Users can accept, reject, or defer recommendations.

3. Forecasting and Trend Analysis
- The system can surface trend summaries for campaigns and accounts.
- Forecasted insights are visible in reporting or dashboard summaries.
- Trend data updates without breaking existing workflow.

4. Shared Assets and Template Intelligence
- Templates and reusable assets can be saved, loaded, and reused reliably.
- Shared assets remain organized and accessible.
- Template intelligence supports faster campaign creation.

5. Team and Agency Controls
- Team and agency users can access the correct shared resources.
- Permissions and access control remain respected.
- Multi-brand and multi-user workflows operate cleanly.

6. Marketplace / Reusable Component Ideas
- The system can support shared or reusable asset concepts without breaking core workflows.
- Marketplace-style objects remain optional and modular.

7. Guardrails and User Control
- The user remains in control of important actions.
- Alerts, approvals, and confirmations are clear.
- Risky or expensive actions should require explicit user confirmation.

8. Stability and Usability
- Core workflows remain fast and simple.
- Existing V1 behavior continues to work.
- The system remains easy to understand and edit.

Definition of Done:
- Phase 20 features are documented, testable, and aligned with Xspresso's core promise.
- The system supports deeper orchestration without sacrificing simplicity or user control.
- New Phase 20 concepts do not break the core V1 workflow.
