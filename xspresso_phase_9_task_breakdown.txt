Xspresso Phase 9 — Task Breakdown

1) Deeper autonomous optimization
- Detect underperforming ads and campaigns automatically
- Suggest the next best improvement
- Prioritize title, hook, angle, and CTA changes
- Offer one-click regeneration

2) Stronger cross-campaign insights
- Compare campaign performance side by side
- Identify patterns across offers and platforms
- Surface what is working and what is not
- Recommend where to focus next

3) Multi-brand intelligence
- Track performance by brand or account
- Separate insights by client, product, or workspace
- Summarize brand-level wins and problems
- Keep reports organized by brand

4) Scheduled summaries
- Daily summary emails
- Weekly performance digest
- Monthly usage and results report
- Action-oriented recommendations in each summary

5) Team collaboration improvements
- Shared notes and comments
- Shared campaign review
- Approval / rejection workflow
- Role-aware access and visibility

6) Template / asset workflow refinements
- Better template browsing and filtering
- Faster save / reuse / duplicate flow
- Better asset organization
- Easier restore from library

7) Marketplace ideas
- Optional template marketplace
- Reusable campaign packs
- Team-approved assets
- Future expansion for shared structures

8) Forecasting
- Predict likely campaign trends
- Estimate performance direction
- Flag risk before money is wasted
- Suggest when to tweak or pause

9) Better credit intelligence
- Explain where credits are going
- Show cost vs return more clearly
- Warn when a feature is becoming too expensive
- Suggest upgrades or changes when usage is high

10) Success criteria
- Xspresso gives smarter optimization guidance
- Users understand performance across campaigns
- Teams can collaborate more easily
- Templates and assets are easier to manage
- Credits and usage are clearer
- The system helps users save time and avoid waste
