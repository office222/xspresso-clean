Xspresso Board Shell Launch and CTA Notes

Purpose:
This file captures the final board shell message and call-to-action guidance for the Xspresso business card and header area.

Core message:
- Xspresso is done-for-you marketing.
- Xspresso gets responses.
- Xspresso helps users stay in command.
- We do the work.
- You enjoy the benefits.

Recommended CTA buttons:
- Try the Demo
- See Pricing
- Start Now
- View Features

CTA rules:
- Keep CTAs short and action-focused.
- Use only two primary buttons in the hero area.
- Make the strongest CTA visually dominant.
- Keep the copy consistent with the done-for-you promise.
- Avoid cluttering the board shell with too many buttons.

Short support lines:
- Wake up your marketing.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.

Layout reminders:
- Keep the cup left of the name.
- Keep the background white behind the logo.
- Let the logo shrink cleanly on smaller screens.
- Avoid stacking the logo image under the text image.
- Keep the board shell clean and premium.

Acceptance checks:
- Logo stays inline on resize.
- CTA buttons remain readable and spaced apart.
- Main message is visible without scrolling too far.
- The board shell feels clean, simple, and confident.
