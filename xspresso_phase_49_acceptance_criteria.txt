Xspresso Phase 49 Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- Campaign creation and launch flows are stable and easy to use.
- Monitoring and alert logic works reliably.
- Billing and credit clarity are consistent and visible.
- Template and library workflows are polished.
- Dashboard views are clear and actionable.
- Support and help text are clean and helpful.
- Users can move through the product without confusion.
- Guardrails prevent bad states and protect the user.
- Core end-to-end Xspresso workflows behave as expected.
