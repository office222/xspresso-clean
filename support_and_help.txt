4. FAQ Draft
Q: What is Xspresso?
A: Xspresso is a done-for-you marketing system that helps users create, monitor, and improve campaigns faster.

Q: Do I need to be a marketing expert?
A: No. Xspresso is designed to help you move faster without stitching together many tools.

Q: Are all features locked by plan?
A: No. All plans include every tool. Credits simply determine fair usage.

Q: What happens if an ad is underperforming?
A: Xspresso warns you and recommends a new title, hook, angle, or regeneration before more credits are spent.

Q: What happens when I switch platforms?
A: Xspresso warns you that monitoring on the old platform will stop unless you choose a monitoring option.

Q: Can I reuse my own campaigns?
A: Yes. You can save, duplicate, export, and import your own work.
