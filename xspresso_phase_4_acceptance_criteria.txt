Xspresso Phase 4 Acceptance Criteria

Purpose:
Define what success looks like for Phase 4, when Xspresso expands into team workflows, integrations, advanced reporting, and agency-scale productivity.

Phase 4 is complete when:
- Team and agency workflows are supported cleanly.
- Integrations connect without confusing setup.
- Advanced reporting is useful and easy to read.
- More automation reduces manual steps.
- Template expansion helps users move faster.
- Library improvements make reuse easier.
- Productivity and scale features work reliably.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Team workflows
- Users can work in shared or multi-user setups.
- Permissions and access remain clear.
- Activity is traceable.

2. Integrations
- External connections work as expected.
- Sync behavior is clear.
- Failures produce helpful messages.

3. Advanced reporting
- Reports summarize performance clearly.
- Metrics are understandable at a glance.
- The user can identify what is working and what needs attention.

4. More automation
- Repetitive work is reduced.
- Automation does not remove user control.
- Users can approve, tweak, or disable automation.

5. Template expansion
- Users can access more templates.
- Templates can be reused and edited.
- Templates remain consistent with Xspresso style and rules.

6. Library improvements
- Users can store, search, and reuse assets more easily.
- Saved items are organized and retrievable.
- Restore and duplicate actions work reliably.

7. Productivity and scale
- The system remains usable as user volume grows.
- Key actions stay fast.
- The interface does not become cluttered or confusing.

Definition of Done:
- Phase 4 features are stable.
- The user experience remains simple.
- The product still feels done-for-you, fast, and in the user's control.

Guiding rule:
Xspresso should always help the user move faster without making the system feel more complicated.
