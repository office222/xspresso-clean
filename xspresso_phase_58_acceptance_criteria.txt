Xspresso Phase 58 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:

- Campaign creation and launch flows are stable and usable end to end.
- Monitoring and alert behavior works reliably.
- Billing and credit clarity are easy for users to understand.
- Template, library, and reuse workflows function cleanly.
- Dashboard and reporting views are clear and actionable.
- Onboarding, help text, and tooltips are finished and consistent.
- QA checks pass for core user flows.
- The product is ready for final release packaging and handoff.
