Xspresso Release Notes

Version: V1 Build Notes
Date: 2026-10-07

Core Positioning
- Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
- We do the work. You enjoy the benefits.
- All plans include every tool. Credits simply determine fair usage.

What V1 Includes
- Campaign creation from product photo, URL, or offer details
- AI-generated ads, headlines, hooks, landing page copy, cart copy, lead magnet copy, and follow-up emails
- Platform-specific ad adaptation
- Lead capture and autoresponder follow-up
- Ad monitoring and alerts
- Underperforming ad regeneration suggestions
- Platform switching warnings and per-ad monitoring choices
- Campaign library, duplication, import/export, templates, and version history
- Dashboard, alerts, activity history, and usage logs
- Billing, subscriptions, credits, and a la carte usage rules

Guiding Rules
- Done-for-you first
- User stays in command
- Fast and simple
- More responses, less software chaos
- Original, never copy
- Monitor and improve
- Save time and money
- Built for people who need to start smart without wasting money

Planned V2 Additions
- Sales letters
- VSL scripts
- Advanced long-form funnels
- Video production support
- Deeper automation and stronger content production

Build Status Notes
- Hero copy has been cleaned up
- Logo/header layout has been fixed so the logo stays inline and shrinks better
- Pricing and credits model emphasize fair usage, not feature bait-and-switch
- Monitoring and alerts should recommend action before more credits are spent on underperforming ads
- Campaign templates, duplication, and version history are part of the reusable workflow
