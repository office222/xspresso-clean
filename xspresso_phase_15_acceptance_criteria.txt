Xspresso Phase 15 Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- Deeper autonomous optimization works reliably.
- Multi-brand coordination works across multiple campaigns.
- Cross-campaign learning improves recommendations.
- Recommendation loops produce useful next actions.
- Forecasting and trend analysis are visible and understandable.
- Advanced workflow orchestration connects major product areas.
- Team and agency controls behave correctly.
- Shared templates and marketplace ideas can be managed cleanly.
- Integrations and automation hooks work as expected.
- Safety and user-control guardrails remain intact.
- Users can still approve, tweak, and stay in command.
- The product remains original, helpful, and easy to use.

Definition of done:
- The phase adds real value without making the product confusing.
- All new features support the core promise of Xspresso.
- The user still feels in control while Xspresso does the work.
