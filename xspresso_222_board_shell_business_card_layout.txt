Xspresso Board Shell Business Card Layout

Purpose:
- Keep the brand header clean, simple, and responsive.
- Keep the cup icon left of the name.
- Prevent wrapping or stacking when the page shrinks.

Layout rules:
- Use a two-cell inline table for the logo area.
- Put the cup image in the first cell.
- Put the name/logo text image in the second cell.
- Keep both images on the same line.
- Use a white background behind the header.
- Use display:block on both images.
- Use max-width and height:auto so the images shrink cleanly.

Responsive behavior:
- On smaller screens, the cup must remain left of the name.
- The name should shrink proportionally.
- The header must not force the cup above the name.
- Avoid hard-coded widths that break inline alignment.

Suggested structure:
- White top bar
- Left: cup image
- Right: name/logo text image
- Optional right-side spacer cell

Acceptance checks:
- Cup stays left of name on desktop.
- Cup stays left of name on tablet.
- Cup stays left of name on mobile.
- Header background remains white.
- No stacking unless screen width is extremely narrow.

Copy guidance:
- Keep the header clean.
- Do not add extra hero text to the header.
- Let the business card component carry the message.

Implementation reminder:
- FrontPage-friendly table layout is preferred.
- Keep the markup simple.
- Avoid nested blocks that force wrapping.
