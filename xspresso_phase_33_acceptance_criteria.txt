Xspresso Phase 33 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is accepted when the following are true:

- Deeper orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports is working.
- Smarter cross-campaign learning and trend detection are working.
- Stronger recommendation loops are working.
- Broader forecasting and performance analysis are working.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence is working.
- Shared assets and template marketplace refinement is working.
- Improved workflow coordination is working.
- Tighter guardrails and user control are working.
- Clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance are working.
- Dashboard, monitoring, and alerts continue to feel polished and consistent.
- The user can understand what Xspresso is doing and what to do next.
- The product still stays aligned with the core promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, with the user in command.
