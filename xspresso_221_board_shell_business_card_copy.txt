Xspresso Board Shell Business Card Copy

Purpose:
A short, clean business card block for the board shell that keeps the cup left of the name, stays readable as the page shrinks, and reinforces the core Xspresso message.

Recommended copy:
- Xspresso — Done-for-you marketing
- Wake up your marketing.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.

Short support line options:
- Done-for-you marketing that gets responses.
- More for less, so you can get your time back.
- Build faster without the software chaos.

Layout guidance:
- Keep the cup image left of the name logo.
- Use a white background behind the logo area.
- Allow the logo to shrink cleanly on smaller screens.
- Prevent the cup and name from stacking unless absolutely necessary.
- Keep the copy short so the card feels premium and easy to scan.

Button ideas:
- Try the Demo
- See Pricing
- Learn More

Best rule:
Keep the business card simple, clean, and readable. It should feel like a polished summary of Xspresso, not a second hero section.
