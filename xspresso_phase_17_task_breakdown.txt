Xspresso Phase 17 — Task Breakdown

1) Orchestration across modules
- Coordinate campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, and templates.
- Keep campaign state, credits, alerts, and follow-up in sync.
- Surface one clear next action for the user.

2) Smarter recommendation loops
- Recommend tweaks for underperforming ads.
- Suggest new titles, hooks, angles, and CTAs.
- Recommend when to regenerate instead of continuing to spend credits.

3) Forecasting and trend analysis
- Track usage trends over time.
- Highlight rising CPC, weak CTR, and conversion shifts.
- Show simple forecast notes for campaign health.

4) Multi-team / multi-brand controls
- Support separate workspaces for brands or clients.
- Keep permissions and defaults isolated by workspace.
- Let users switch between brands cleanly.

5) Shared templates and marketplace ideas
- Let users save, reuse, and share templates.
- Support internal team libraries.
- Prepare for future marketplace-style sharing.

6) Guardrails and user control
- Keep users in command.
- Require approval before launch.
- Keep all important actions visible and reversible where practical.
- Stay aligned with the "done-for-you, never copy" rule.

7) Success criteria
- Users can manage more than one brand or workflow.
- Xspresso can coordinate multiple modules without confusion.
- Recommendations are actionable and easy to understand.
- Team and marketplace concepts remain controlled and safe.
