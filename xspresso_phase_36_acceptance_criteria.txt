Xspresso Phase 36 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- Final orchestration polish works across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Cross-campaign learning is active and produces useful recommendations.
- Recommendation loops are working and suggest the next best action.
- Forecasting and performance analysis are visible in a usable way.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence works without confusion.
- Template marketplace refinement behaves correctly.
- Workflow coordination is stable.
- Guardrails and user control are respected.
- Credit intelligence and usage guidance are clear.
- Dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and launch-flow polish feel consistent and usable.
- The system remains aligned with the core promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, while the user stays in command.
