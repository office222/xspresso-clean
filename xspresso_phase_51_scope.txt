Xspresso Phase 51 Scope

Purpose:
Final polish, consolidation, and readiness for handoff/release.

Covers:
- full V1 feature consolidation
- end-to-end flow validation
- dashboard clarity and usability polish
- monitoring, alerts, and underperforming ad guidance
- billing, credits, and a la carte usage clarity
- platform switching rules and warning behavior
- template, library, and reuse workflow refinement
- onboarding, help text, and tooltips cleanup
- support and release packaging
- final QA and release readiness checks

Success criteria:
- the product feels cohesive
- core user flow is clear and stable
- credits and usage behavior are easy to understand
- monitoring and alerts point to next actions
- the release package is ready for implementation and handoff
