CURRENT CORE POSITIONING
- Xspresso is done-for-you marketing.
- Users stay in command.
- Xspresso does the heavy lifting.
- All plans include every tool.
- Credits determine fair usage.
- Xspresso helps users create, launch, monitor, and improve campaigns faster.
- Xspresso avoids copying and stays strategy-first and original.

BUILD PRINCIPLES
1. Done-for-you first.
2. Keep the user in command.
3. Every tool available to every plan.
4. Credits control usage, not access.
5. Warn early, recommend fixes, and offer regeneration.
6. Save time, reduce software chaos, and improve results.
7. Keep campaigns editable, reusable, and versioned.
8. Support the full funnel: page, ad, lead capture, follow-up, monitoring.
9. Keep the copy simple, clear, and customer-focused.
10. Never copy other people’s work; only reuse the user’s own campaigns.
