Xspresso Board Shell Final Release Notes

Purpose:
This file captures the final release-ready rules for the Xspresso board shell so the header, business card, CTA area, and footer all behave consistently.

Final requirements:
- White header background.
- Cup stays left of the name/logo.
- Logo shrinks cleanly on smaller screens.
- No wrapping or stacking that puts the cup above the name unless the screen is extremely narrow.
- Business card copy stays readable.
- CTA buttons stay clear, simple, and visible.
- Footer remains minimal and premium.
- Layout stays FrontPage-friendly.
- Keep the Xspresso-only naming convention.

Acceptance checklist:
- The logo appears inline on desktop and tablet.
- The logo scales down on narrow screens.
- The header never looks dark or heavy.
- The business card section remains readable at all sizes.
- Primary and secondary CTAs are easy to spot.
- The shell looks clean, polished, and release-ready.

Core message to preserve:
Done-for-you marketing. You stay in command. We do the work.
