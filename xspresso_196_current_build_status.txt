Xspresso Current Build Status

This file captures the current state of the Xspresso project after the first major build pass.

Core Positioning
- Done-for-you marketing, fast.
- Xspresso is not about more software.
- It helps users get more responses, build a list, and follow up automatically.
- The user stays in command while Xspresso does the heavy lifting.
- We do the work. You enjoy the benefits.

V1 Focus
- Campaign creation from a product photo, URL, or offer details
- AI-generated ads, headlines, hooks, landing pages, carts, lead magnets, and follow-up
- Platform adaptation for social uploads
- Ad monitoring and alerts
- Lead capture and follow-up automation
- Credits and fair-usage billing
- Dashboard, alerts, and activity history
- Library, templates, export/import, and reuse of the user’s own work

Platform and Monitoring Rules
- All plans include every tool.
- Credits determine fair usage.
- Monitoring charges can be daily, weekly, or monthly.
- Switching platforms must show a strong warning.
- Users can keep specific ads running, move them to their library, or keep monitoring them for credits.
- Underperforming ads should prompt regeneration before more credits are spent.

Campaign Flow
1. User adds offer details.
2. Xspresso builds a campaign brief.
3. Xspresso generates copy and assets.
4. User reviews, edits, approves, or regenerates.
5. User launches.
6. Xspresso monitors and alerts.
7. User improves or regenerates as needed.

V2 Ideas
- Sales letters
- VSLs
- Video production
- Deeper optimization
- Advanced cloning of the user’s own campaigns

Best Current Messaging
- Wake up your marketing.
- More responses. Less software chaos.
- You stay in command. We do the work.
- Xspresso gives you more for less, so you can get your time back.
- Don’t buy another job. Start building a lifestyle.
