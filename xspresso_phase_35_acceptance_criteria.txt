Xspresso Phase 35 Acceptance Criteria

- Orchestration and optimization work across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
- Cross-campaign learning and trend detection are working.
- Recommendation loops surface helpful next actions.
- Forecasting and performance analysis are visible and usable.
- Multi-brand and multi-team intelligence is supported.
- Shared asset and template marketplace refinement is functional.
- Guardrails and user control remain clear and reliable.
- Credit intelligence and usage guidance are accurate and easy to understand.
- Dashboard, monitoring, and alerts remain polished and consistent.
- The Phase 35 definition of done is met when all core flows work together cleanly.
