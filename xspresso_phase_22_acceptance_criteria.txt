Xspresso Phase 22 — Acceptance Criteria

1) Deeper autonomous orchestration works across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports.
2) Cross-campaign learning improves recommendations over time.
3) Recommendation loops produce clearer, more useful next steps.
4) Multi-brand / multi-team intelligence works without breaking user control.
5) Workflow coordination remains consistent across the core modules.
6) Credit intelligence gives accurate usage guidance and upgrade suggestions.
7) Guardrails and user control remain intact.
8) The system stays understandable, editable, and simple to use.
9) Phase 22 is considered complete when the above areas work together reliably and the user can launch, monitor, improve, and manage campaigns with less manual effort.
