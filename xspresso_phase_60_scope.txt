Xspresso Phase 60 — Scope

This phase covers the final stabilization and long-term polish layer for Xspresso.

Scope:
- final orchestration refinement across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, reports, and credits
- cross-campaign learning improvements
- smarter recommendation tuning
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team workflow refinement
- template and asset marketplace cleanup
- dashboard clarity and usability polish
- monitoring and alert reliability improvements
- onboarding, help text, and tooltip cleanup
- release hardening, support packaging, and handoff readiness

Success criteria:
- the core V1 workflow is stable and clear
- monitoring and alerts are easy to understand
- billing and credit usage are transparent
- templates, library, and reuse flow smoothly
- dashboard and reporting feel usable and predictable
- the product is ready for sustained real-world use
