Xspresso Phase 13 — Task Breakdown

Purpose:
This phase focuses on the final orchestration layer of Xspresso, where the system starts coordinating campaigns, monitoring, leads, alerts, billing, and recommendations more intelligently across the whole app.

1) Orchestration engine
- Coordinate actions across campaigns, ads, leads, alerts, credits, and billing.
- Keep modules in sync so the app feels unified.
- Prioritize the most important next action for the user.

2) Cross-module intelligence
- Learn from campaign performance.
- Connect alert trends to campaign changes.
- Connect billing usage to platform actions.
- Connect lead behavior to follow-up sequences.

3) Smarter recommendations
- Recommend campaign improvements.
- Recommend ad regeneration when performance drops.
- Recommend budget/credit adjustments.
- Recommend upgrade opportunities only when they reduce cost or add clear value.

4) Scheduled summaries
- Build daily and weekly summaries.
- Show campaign health at a glance.
- Show credit usage at a glance.
- Show alerts, wins, and underperforming items clearly.

5) Workflow coordination
- Help the user move from draft to launch.
- Help the user move from alert to fix.
- Help the user move from lead to follow-up.
- Help the user move from low performance to regeneration.

6) Safety and guardrails
- Keep all actions explainable.
- Keep the user in command.
- Require approvals for sensitive changes.
- Preserve the never-copy rule.
- Avoid hidden actions and surprise usage.

7) Success criteria
- Xspresso can coordinate actions across the product without confusing the user.
- Recommendations feel useful and timely.
- The dashboard shows a clear next step.
- Credits, monitoring, alerts, and billing all feel connected.
- The product still feels fast, simple, and done-for-you.
