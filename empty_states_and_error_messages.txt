Xspresso V1 — Empty States and Error Messages

Purpose:
Keep the product helpful when users have no data yet or something goes wrong.

Core rule:
Always explain what happened and give the user a next step.

--------------------------------------------------
EMPTY STATES
--------------------------------------------------

1) No campaigns yet
Message:
You don’t have any campaigns yet.

Helpful line:
Start by adding a product photo, URL, or a few details about your offer.

Action:
Create Campaign

2) No ads yet
Message:
You don’t have any ads yet.

Helpful line:
Xspresso can build your first ad set when you add your offer details.

Action:
Build Ads

3) No alerts yet
Message:
No alerts right now.

Helpful line:
Xspresso is keeping an eye on your campaigns.

Action:
View Dashboard

4) No leads yet
Message:
No leads captured yet.

Helpful line:
Once your campaign is live, leads will appear here.

Action:
View Campaigns

5) No credits left
Message:
You are out of credits for this cycle.

Helpful line:
Add credits or upgrade your plan to keep working.

Action:
Add Credits / Upgrade Plan

--------------------------------------------------
ERROR MESSAGES
--------------------------------------------------

1) Missing offer details
Message:
Xspresso needs a little more information to build your campaign.

Helpful line:
Add a product name, URL, image, or short description.

Action:
Continue Setup

2) Invalid URL
Message:
That URL doesn’t look right.

Helpful line:
Check the link and try again.

Action:
Fix URL

3) Platform connection failed
Message:
Xspresso could not connect to that platform.

Helpful line:
Please check your account permissions and try again.

Action:
Reconnect Platform

4) Monitoring paused
Message:
Monitoring is paused for this platform.

Helpful line:
Resume monitoring to keep tracking your ads.

Action:
Resume Monitoring

5) Regeneration failed
Message:
Xspresso could not regenerate this section right now.

Helpful line:
Try again or edit the section manually.

Action:
Try Again

6) Launch blocked
Message:
This campaign is not ready to launch.

Helpful line:
Please review the missing items and fix them first.

Action:
Review Missing Items

--------------------------------------------------
FALLBACK COPY
--------------------------------------------------

If something unexpected happens:
Message:
Something went wrong.

Helpful line:
Please try again. If the problem continues, check your settings or contact support.

Action:
Try Again

--------------------------------------------------
STYLE GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------

- Keep error messages short.
- Tell the user what happened.
- Tell the user what to do next.
- Keep the tone calm and helpful.
- Avoid technical jargon unless needed.
- Make every error feel recoverable.

--------------------------------------------------
ONE-LINE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------

Xspresso should always show a clear next step when the user hits an empty state or error.
