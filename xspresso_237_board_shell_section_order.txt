Xspresso Board Shell Section Order

Purpose:
Keep the front board shell clean, readable, and premium while preserving the Xspresso brand message.

Recommended Order:
1. Top header / logo row
   - White background
   - Cup logo left of text logo
   - Shrinks cleanly on smaller screens
   - Never stacks cup on top of name

2. Hero / primary message
   - Xspresso — Done-for-you marketing
   - Wake up your marketing
   - More responses. Less software chaos.
   - You stay in command. We do the work.

3. Business card component
   - Short promise
   - Quick supporting bullets
   - Demo and pricing CTA buttons
   - Keep this section visually simple

4. CTA row
   - Primary button: Try the Demo
   - Secondary button: See Pricing
   - Keep spacing clear and premium

5. Supporting section / bullets
   - Done-for-you benefits
   - More responses
   - List building
   - Follow-up automation
   - Less software juggling

6. Footer / support links
   - Help center
   - Billing help
   - Platform monitoring help
   - Platform switching help
   - Keep footer small and simple

Responsive Rules:
- Header must stay inline on desktop and mobile where possible.
- The cup must remain left of the name.
- White background behind the logo and header area.
- Images should shrink rather than wrap awkwardly.
- The layout should remain FrontPage-friendly.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Looks clean at full width.
- Logo remains inline and readable.
- No dark background behind logo.
- CTA buttons are easy to see.
- Business card is readable without clutter.
- The shell feels premium, simple, and fast.

Key Message:
Xspresso is done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
