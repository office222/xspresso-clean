Xspresso Phase 32 Scope

This phase covers:
- further refinement of autonomous orchestration across campaigns, leads, alerts, billing, templates, and reports
- stronger cross-campaign learning and trend detection
- more advanced recommendation loops
- broader forecasting and performance analysis
- multi-brand and multi-team intelligence improvements
- shared assets and template marketplace refinement
- improved workflow coordination and scheduling
- tighter guardrails and user control
- clearer credit intelligence and usage guidance
- continued polish for dashboard, monitoring, alerts, and reporting

Success criteria:
- the system remains simple for the user
- recommendations are useful and actionable
- alerts are clear and timely
- credits and usage remain easy to understand
- multi-brand workflows remain organized
- guardrails keep the product safe and controllable
