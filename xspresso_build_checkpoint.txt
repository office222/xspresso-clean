Xspresso Build Checkpoint

Current direction:
- Xspresso is a done-for-you marketing system.
- Core promise: more responses, less software chaos.
- User stays in command; Xspresso does the heavy lifting.
- All plans include every tool; credits determine fair usage.

V1 focus:
- Campaign builder
- Campaign generation
- Platform adaptation
- Lead capture and follow-up
- Monitoring and alerts
- Underperforming ad regeneration
- Credits and usage engine
- Platform switch flow
- Campaign library, duplicate, export/import
- Dashboard and campaign cards
- Billing, renewals, and upgrade suggestions

Key copy lines:
- Xspresso isn’t about more software. It’s about done-for-you marketing that gets responses, builds your list, follows up automatically, and lets you stay in command.
- We do the work. You enjoy the benefits.
- Improve before spending more credits monitoring an ad that is not performing.
- Let Xspresso regenerate this ad with a new title, hook, or angle.

Build principles:
- Done-for-you first
- Never copy, only inspired-by strategy
- Fast, simple, and customer-focused
- Clear warnings and helpful next actions
- Keep everything editable and recoverable
