Xspresso Board Shell Layout Cleanup Notes

Purpose:
Keep the front board shell clean, premium, and responsive while preserving the core Xspresso message.

Layout rules:
- Keep the cup icon left of the Xspresso name logo.
- Use a white background behind the logo area.
- Prevent the cup and name from stacking on smaller screens.
- Shrink the logo set gracefully instead of letting it wrap.
- Keep the top header simple and readable.

Visual priorities:
- White header background.
- Orange as an accent only.
- Clean spacing around the logo and business card.
- Strong hierarchy: logo first, hero second, CTA third.

Business card block:
- Keep the card short and readable.
- Emphasize done-for-you marketing.
- Reinforce: You stay in command. We do the work.
- Keep the CTA buttons visible and uncluttered.

Responsive checks:
- Logo remains inline on tablet and desktop.
- Logo scales down on mobile.
- No overlap, no stacking, no wrap surprises.
- CTA buttons remain easy to tap.

Acceptance checklist:
- Header is white.
- Cup stays left of the name.
- Name/logo stays inline.
- Page feels clean and premium.
- The board shell matches the Xspresso brand tone.
