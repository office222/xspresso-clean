Xspresso Phase 4 Scope

Purpose:
Phase 4 expands Xspresso beyond the core done-for-you workflow into a more mature product ecosystem.

Focus:
- Team and agency workflows
- Stronger integrations
- Better reporting and exports
- More automation controls
- More templates and presets
- More advanced campaign management

Potential Phase 4 Features:
1. Team Workspaces
- Shared campaign spaces
- User roles and permissions
- Review and approval workflows for teams
- Shared library items and templates

2. Integrations
- Email platforms
- CRM connections
- Webhooks
- Zap-style automation hooks
- Import/export with more tools

3. Advanced Reporting
- Deeper performance summaries
- Trend charts
- Campaign comparisons
- Credit usage by feature
- Exportable reports

4. More Automation
- Rule-based actions
- Auto-pause rules
- Auto-regenerate suggestions
- Auto-follow-up branching
- Scheduled campaign actions

5. Template Expansion
- More vertical-specific templates
- Industry presets
- Seasonal campaigns
- Offer-type presets
- Multi-step funnel templates

6. Library and Reuse Improvements
- Better version tagging
- Searchable library
- Filters and categories
- Favorites and pinned campaigns
- Restore and duplicate shortcuts

7. Productivity and Scale
- Bulk actions
- Bulk uploads
- Bulk exports
- Multi-campaign dashboards
- Agency-friendly management tools

Guardrails:
- Keep the product easy to understand
- Do not hide core tools behind confusing feature walls
- Keep credits and usage clear
- Keep the user in command
- Keep the done-for-you promise intact
- Avoid adding complexity that hurts the fast/simple feel

Definition of Success:
Phase 4 should make Xspresso more powerful for teams and scaling users without losing the clarity and simplicity of V1 and V2.

Summary:
Phase 4 is about scaling Xspresso into a more complete platform for teams, agencies, and power users while keeping the original promise: done-for-you marketing, fast, simple, and under user control.
