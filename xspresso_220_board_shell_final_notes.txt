Xspresso Board Shell Final Notes

Purpose:
- Keep the cup image left of the name logo.
- Keep the header background white.
- Ensure the logo scales down cleanly on smaller screens.
- Prevent the cup and name from stacking or overlapping.
- Keep the layout FrontPage-friendly and simple to maintain.

Recommended rules:
1. Use a two-cell inline table for the logo.
2. Put the cup in the first cell and the name image in the second cell.
3. Use white-space:nowrap on the logo row to prevent wrapping.
4. Use display:block on both images to remove spacing gaps.
5. Use max-width and height:auto so the text logo can shrink safely.
6. Keep the top bar background white, with orange used only as an accent.

Responsive behavior:
- On wider screens, the cup and logo should sit on the same line.
- On narrow screens, the images should scale down instead of stacking.
- If needed, use a smaller source image for the text logo rather than letting it stretch.

Acceptance checks:
- Cup stays left of the name.
- No overlap when the browser is resized.
- No dark background behind the logo.
- The header remains clean and premium.
- The board shell looks consistent with the rest of Xspresso.
