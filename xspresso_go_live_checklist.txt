Xspresso Go-Live Checklist

Purpose:
A final checklist to make sure the first public release of Xspresso is ready, consistent, and easy to launch.

Checklist:
1. Brand and messaging
- Confirm the core promise is consistent everywhere.
- Make sure the hero says done-for-you marketing clearly.
- Keep the message focused on more responses, less software chaos, and the user staying in command.

2. Product scope
- Confirm V1 includes campaign creation.
- Confirm V1 includes ad generation.
- Confirm V1 includes landing pages, shopping cart, or lead capture.
- Confirm V1 includes follow-up.
- Confirm V1 includes monitoring and alerts.
- Confirm V1 includes credits and usage rules.

3. Dashboard and workflow
- Confirm the dashboard shows active campaigns, alerts, credits remaining, and monitored ads.
- Confirm campaign cards show performance and quick actions.
- Confirm users can approve, edit, regenerate, and launch from one flow.

4. Monitoring and alerts
- Confirm underperforming ads trigger helpful alerts.
- Confirm alerts recommend a next step.
- Confirm platform switch warnings are clear.
- Confirm monitoring costs are visible before confirmation.

5. Billing and usage
- Confirm all plans include every tool.
- Confirm credits determine usage.
- Confirm a la carte items are visible.
- Confirm upgrade suggestions appear when usage makes it sensible.

6. Lead capture and follow-up
- Confirm leads are captured correctly.
- Confirm follow-up sequences are connected to the right campaign.
- Confirm lead scoring and list routing work as expected.

7. Templates and library
- Confirm users can save campaigns to the library.
- Confirm users can duplicate their own work.
- Confirm templates are easy to start from.
- Confirm restore and version history work.

8. Launch readiness
- Confirm no required fields are missing.
- Confirm preview screens render correctly.
- Confirm launch confirmation is clear.
- Confirm post-launch monitoring starts correctly.

9. Support and safety
- Confirm error messages are friendly and useful.
- Confirm help text is in place.
- Confirm privacy, consent, and original-use guardrails are clear.

10. Final pass
- Test on the primary browsers.
- Test a small mobile view.
- Test one full end-to-end campaign.
- Fix anything that feels confusing, slow, or unfinished.

Release Rule:
If the product is clear, usable, and consistent with the core promise, it is ready to go live.
