Xspresso Phase 9 Scope

Purpose:
Phase 9 expands Xspresso into a more intelligent, scalable marketing system with stronger automation, deeper learning, and broader collaboration.

Scope:
- Deeper autonomous optimization suggestions
- Stronger cross-campaign insights
- Better multi-brand intelligence
- Smarter recommendations based on historical results
- Scheduled summaries and executive-style reporting
- Team collaboration improvements
- Shared asset and template workflow refinements
- Expanded marketplace / template exchange ideas
- Better forecasting and trend analysis
- More detailed credit intelligence and usage guidance

Guardrails:
- Keep the user in command
- Never copy other people's work
- Keep every recommendation editable
- Explain why a recommendation is being made
- Keep the workflow fast and simple
- Preserve fair usage and transparency

Success Criteria:
- Xspresso can recommend smarter next steps using performance history
- Users can see clearer multi-brand patterns
- Teams can collaborate with less friction
- Reporting is easier to understand at a glance
- Templates and assets can be reused more efficiently
- The system still feels simple, fast, and done-for-you
