Xspresso Phase 26 — Acceptance Criteria

This phase is complete when:
- Final refinement and stabilization are in place.
- Workflow polish is complete across campaign creation, review, launch, monitoring, and alerts.
- Recommendations are clear, helpful, and actionable.
- Cross-campaign intelligence works consistently.
- Team and multi-brand coordination behaves as expected.
- Credit and usage clarity is visible to the user.
- Guardrails and user control are preserved.
- No major blocking issues remain in the core V1/V2 workflow.
- The product feels consistent, simple, and stable.

Definition of done:
Xspresso is stable, polished, easy to use, and ready for the next expansion stage.
