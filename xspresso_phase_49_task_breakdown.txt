Xspresso Phase 49 — Task Breakdown

1) Final stabilization
- Review all core V1 flows for consistency.
- Confirm campaign creation, launch, monitoring, and alerts all behave predictably.
- Tighten edge cases and fallback behavior.

2) Campaign flow polish
- Validate offer input, brief creation, generation, review, and launch steps.
- Ensure edit / approve / regenerate actions are clear and fast.
- Confirm saved drafts and version history work correctly.

3) Monitoring and alerts
- Tune ad health checks.
- Verify underperforming ad warnings are easy to understand.
- Confirm the regenerate recommendation flow is visible and helpful.
- Check rising CPC and low CTR alert thresholds.

4) Billing and credit clarity
- Confirm plan limits, credit usage, and a la carte charges are shown clearly.
- Verify upgrade suggestions appear only when usage makes sense.
- Make sure the credits remaining display is accurate.

5) Template and library workflow
- Test save, reuse, duplicate, export, and import flows.
- Verify campaign versions and templates are easy to restore.
- Make sure users can start from proven work without confusion.

6) Dashboard clarity
- Tighten campaign cards, status badges, and quick actions.
- Confirm alerts, credits, and monitored ads are visible at a glance.
- Make sure the dashboard always points to a next action.

7) Support and help content
- Review empty states, tooltips, and error messages.
- Make help text shorter, friendlier, and more action-oriented.
- Keep the tone aligned with done-for-you, in-command messaging.

8) Success criteria
- Users can build, review, launch, and monitor campaigns without confusion.
- Credits and usage are understood clearly.
- Alerts recommend the next best action.
- The app feels stable, fast, and easy to control.
